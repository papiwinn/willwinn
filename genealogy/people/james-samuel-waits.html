James Samuel “J. S.” Waits

c. 1830/31 – after 1900 · Newberry Co., SC → Lauderdale Co., TN
FACT father of J. C. Waits per Find a Grave (via William) · HYPOTHESIS (strong) identification with the census “J S Waits”
Photo placeholder

Relation: Father of James Clayborn Waits (linked on J. C.’s Find a Grave memorial #200803433); husband of Almenia Earhart Waits. William’s 2nd-great-grandfather.

In the censuses he is “J S Wates” (1850), “J S Waits” (1860), “Samuel Waits” (1869, 1870) and “J S Waits” (1900) (FACT (index)). The initials match Find a Grave’s “James Samuel”; with the wife’s name and J. C.’s household beside his in 1900, the identification is HYPOTHESIS (strong).

Timeline

  • FACT (index) 1850: Newberry Co., SC — “J S Wates” (b. ~1831), in the household that follows Mark and Mahala Wates
  • FACT Wife: Almenia — “Almena” (1860), “Almma” (1870), “A E” (1900). Marriage (between 1850 and about 1857) NOT FOUND
  • FACT (index) 1860 and 1870: Newberry Co., SC, with Almena/Almma
  • FACT 1869: SC state census, Newberry Township No. 9 — “Samuel Waits”, listed between Mark Waits and Drucilla Waits
  • FACT Civil War: “Samuel Waits, discharged in camp”, Company H, Holcombe Legion, SC (Capt. James M. Maffett) — the entry exists; HYPOTHESIS that it is him (Bluford and Drayton Waits of the same Newberry family served in the same company)
  • HYPOTHESIS Move to Tennessee: between 1870 and 1900, probably the 1870s (J. C. was in Lauderdale “as a boy”, per the 1978 Parker memoir)
  • FACT (index) 1900: Lauderdale Co., TN — “J S Waits” (b. 1830) and “A E” (b. 1832), indexed immediately before J C Waits’s household; HYPOTHESIS (strong) that this places the parents next door to their son
  • FACT Died: after the June 1900 census; HYPOTHESIS probably before 1910 (his son indexed as “James Waits Jr” in 1910). Date and place NOT FOUND
  • NOT FOUND Burial — candidates Maplewood (Ripley) or Green’s Chapel/Halls area. CONFLICT J. C.’s Find a Grave memorial (per William) links him as James Samuel Waits, 1830–1910, Maplewood; Crystal could not open the memorial and found no record of the death or burial
