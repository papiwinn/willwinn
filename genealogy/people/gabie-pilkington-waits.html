Gabie Pilkington Hopper Waits

28 May 1883 – 24 May 1966
Step relation
Photo placeholder

Relation: Second wife of James Clayborn Waits; stepmother to Lovy and Rhoda’s other daughters. Biological mother of Hopper children (not of Lovy).

Gabie Pilkington married into the Waits household after Rhoda’s death. She is named stepmother in the Linnie Chapman Drive clipping. Her biological children are on the Hopper line (son Eddie Lee Hopper; daughter Lillie May Hopper Ray; Iantha (Aintha) Bethel Hopper Beasley is a documented Hopper daughter). Do not list Lovy, Josie Belle, Laura Clemmons, or Katherine Waits as Gabie’s biological Waits children.
