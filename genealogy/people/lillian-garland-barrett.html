Lillian G. (Garland) Barrett

c. 4 Mar 1879 – 15 Nov 1969 · Wrentham, Massachusetts
FACT Richard’s mother (named “Lillian” in 1923 birth record) · HYPOTHESIS (strong) same woman as the 1969 death record
Photo placeholder

Relation: Mother of Richard Clinton Barrett; wife of Royden Y. Barrett; Robyn’s paternal grandmother. FACT (image) Richard’s 1923 Norwood birth record names his parents “Royden Y. and Lillian.”

FACT (image) The Millis 1969 death entry reads “Lillian G. Barrett (Garland)” — the parentheses mark her maiden name. HYPOTHESIS (strong) That she is Richard’s mother: the name, maiden surname, and town all fit; she would have been about 43 at his birth.
