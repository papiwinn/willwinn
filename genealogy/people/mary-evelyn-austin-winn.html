Relation: FACT Mother of Willis Hunley Winn — wife of William H. Winn in the 1880 census, where her children include “W. H.”, 6, and her stepchildren (to William) James M. and Henrietta Pitt. Austin Hunley Winn’s grandmother. Husband: William Hunley Winn. HYPOTHESIS Austin Hunley Winn’s first given name, Austin, matches her maiden name — an inference, and the maiden name itself is from the FS tree only (see Austin’s page).
