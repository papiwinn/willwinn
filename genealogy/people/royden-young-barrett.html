Royden Young “Roy Y.” Barrett

c. 19 Apr 1877 – 12 May 1939 · Millis, Massachusetts
FACT Richard’s father (named in 1923 birth record) · HYPOTHESIS (strong) same man as the 1939 Millis death
Photo placeholder

Relation: Father of Richard Clinton Barrett; Robyn’s paternal grandfather. FACT (image) Richard’s 1923 Norwood birth record names his father “Royden Y.”

HYPOTHESIS (strong) That this Royden Y. is the Royden Y. Barrett who died in Millis on 12 May 1939: the name is rare, a Royden Y. Barrett Jr. existed, and the family was in Millis by 1928. No record found directly states “son of” linking the 1939 death to Richard.
