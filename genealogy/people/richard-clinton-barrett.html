Richard Clinton Barrett

26 Jan 1923 – 29 Apr 1996 · born Islington (Westwood) or Norwood, Massachusetts
FACT Robyn’s father · DISCREPANCY birthplace · died 29 Apr 1996
Photo placeholder

Relation: Father of Robyn Louise Barrett (William’s wife). FACT (William) Richard Clinton Barrett, born 26 January 1923 in Islington (Westwood), Massachusetts. Wife: Elizabeth Jane Holden (married 13 Oct 1945, Medway). FACT (William) He died 29 April 1996.

FACT A lifelong Millis man: high-school football player (1939, 1941), Water Commissioner (1956), Superintendent of Streets from 1 Nov 1960 and later head of Public Works until he resigned on 25 Aug 1976, and a Captain in the Millis Fire Department (1961–68). The 1985 centennial report lists “Dick and Betty Barrett.”

FACT (image) His birth was registered in Norwood: “Births reported in the town of Norwood, 1923” (p.26) reads “Jan. 26, Richard Clinton Barrett, Royden Y. and Lillian.” DISCREPANCY William places the birth in Islington (Westwood); the record was reported in Norwood. Both are shown — not resolved. HYPOTHESIS He may have been born at Norwood Hospital, or the family may have lived in Norwood at the time; the family had been in Norwood since at least 1904.
