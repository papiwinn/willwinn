Willis Hunley Winn

6 Aug 1874 · Tipton Co., TN – 26 Feb 1943 · Cherry, Lauderdale Co., TN
FACT Father’s side · Austin’s father

Relation: Father of Austin Hunley Winn. FACT (William) Full name Willis Hunley Winn — locks letter envelopes Mr. W. H. Winn / Mr & Mrs W. H. Winn, Route 2, Henning, Tennessee.

FACT Grocery merchant at Cherry. Ill February 1943 (letters); died 26 February 1943 at Cherry (death certificate); buried early March 1943, Munford Cemetery, Covington. Spouse Fannie Lee (McBride) Winn (William; Mrs. W. H. Winn in letters). Parker 1978 and 1932 Cherry Willis mentions are secondary corroboration.

FACT Parents: William Hunley Winn and Mary E. (Alston or Austin) Winn — the 1880 census lists him as “W. H.”, 6, son in their household. His brothers and sisters are also named in the 1932 obituary of his sister Mrs. Susan Winn Kinney — see Siblings. His mother’s maiden name unresolved: Alston (death certificate, informant his widow) / Austin (FS tree). HYPOTHESIS (strong) His parents’ exact dates and their 1865 marriage are from the FamilySearch tree.

    • HYPOTHESIS Research lead only: Simon Peter Driver (b. 1842), whose 1930 death abstract gives a Winn mother, was probably a close Winn relative — on this reading, a grandson of Peter Winn. The likely link is that Driver’s mother was one of Peter Winn’s daughters, Louisa J. (b. 1813) or Mary (1815–1880) (FS tree). That would make Driver a nephew of Willis’s father, William Hunley Winn, and so Willis’s first cousin, born 33 years before him. It would explain W. P. Winn being the informant on the certificate, and the family’s use of the name Peter (Peter Winn; Simon Peter Driver; William Peter Winn). Not proven and not on the tree; the certificate image and Driver’s 1930 obituary would test it.
