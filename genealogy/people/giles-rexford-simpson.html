Giles Rexford Simpson

22 Jun 1889 – 9 Apr 1944
FACT Mother’s side · title Rexford · aka Prexford
Photo placeholder

Relation: Husband of Carrie Love “Lovy” Waits Simpson; William’s maternal grandfather on the Simpson line.

Title uses Rexford (Carrie FaG #55032391 spouse link; Rhoda Violet Gonnsen 2019 obit parent naming). Keep Prexford as a documented Maley–Yarbrough FH variant on Carrie’s 1985 abstract.

Birth year 1889 FACT: the Asbury Church Cemetery stone list gives SIMPSON,Giles R. June 22, 1889--April 9, 1944, and the 1900, 1910 and 1920 census indexes all give about 1889. CONFLICT Carrie’s Find a Grave spouse link shows 1899–1944 — an outlier (probably a typo) against the stone plus three censuses.

Parents HYPOTHESIS (strong): Andrew Solon Simpson (1857–1940) — in the 1900 census Giles is indexed inside Andrew’s household. Mother HYPOTHESIS: Andrew’s first wife Kate McMahon. Giles’s 1944 death certificate would test this.
