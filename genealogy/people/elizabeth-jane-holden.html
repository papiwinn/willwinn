Elizabeth Jane “Betty” Holden

2 Aug 1923 – 3 Nov 2015 · died Millis, MA · birthplace unconfirmed
FACT Robyn’s mother · NOT CONFIRMED birthplace · died 3 Nov 2015, Millis
Photo placeholder

Relation: Mother of Robyn Louise Barrett (William’s wife). FACT (William) Elizabeth Jane Holden, born 2 August 1923. Known as “Betty” (Millis town reports, per Crystal). Husband: Richard Clinton Barrett (married 13 Oct 1945, Medway). FACT (William) She died 3 November 2015; FACT the Millis 2015 annual report records her death in Millis, MA, aged 92 — consistent with her 1923 birth.

FACT Living in Medway, MA when she married in 1945; afterwards in Millis — treasurer of a Millis organization as “Mrs. Richard Barrett” (1948), and “Betty Barrett,” ceramics teacher for the Millis Council on Aging (1987–91), and in 2015 an honorary member of its board.

NOT CONFIRMED Birthplace: Eastport, Maine or Blackstone, Massachusetts — both possible. Her mother, Agnes Corbett, was born in Eastport (FACT, William), which may explain the family’s belief in an Eastport birth; her father, William Holden, lived in Blackstone. No birth record found: Crystal checked the Holden Genealogy (1923), archive.org full text, Border Historical Society and Shead High School material; Blackstone town reports for the 1920s are not online. FACT The 1950 US census gives her birthplace as Massachusetts, which supports Blackstone over Eastport — but the birthplace stays NOT CONFIRMED until a birth or marriage record names it.
