Fannie Lee (McBride) Winn

b. 1894 Tipton County, TN
FACT Father’s side · Austin’s mother

Relation: Mother of Austin Hunley Winn. FACT (William) Fannie Lee McBride, later Winn; born 1894 in Tipton County, Tennessee. Slug remains fannie-winn.html. Maiden McBride is locked. FACT (William) parents James Peter McBride & Molly Fulce. Civil marriage to Willis and Fannie’s death/burial remain open on free sources.

Letters address her as Mother / Mrs. W. H. Winn; after Willis’s death Austin asks about mail to Mrs. W. H. or Mrs. Fannie. Parker 1978: “Mrs. Fannie Winn.” Do not confuse with Mary Oletta Littleheart Simpson Winn (Austin’s wife).
