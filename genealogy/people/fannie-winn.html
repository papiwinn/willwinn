Fannie Lee (McBride) Winn

b. 1894 Tipton County, TN – d. 1987 (per FS tree)
FACT Father’s side · Austin’s mother

Relation: Mother of Austin Hunley Winn. FACT (William) Fannie Lee McBride, later Winn; born 1894 in Tipton County, Tennessee. Slug remains fannie-winn.html. Maiden McBride is locked. FACT (William) parents James Peter McBride & Molly Fulce. FACT She married Willis on 11 Jan 1914 in Lauderdale Co., TN (marriage record). HYPOTHESIS Per the FamilySearch tree she died in 1987; her death record and burial remain open.

Letters address her as Mother / Mrs. W. H. Winn; after Willis’s death Austin asks about mail to Mrs. W. H. or Mrs. Fannie. Parker 1978: “Mrs. Fannie Winn.” Do not confuse with Mary Oletta Littleheart Simpson Winn (Austin’s wife).
