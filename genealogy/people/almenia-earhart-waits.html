Almenia “Almena” (Earhart) Waits

c. 1831/32 – after 1900 · Lexington / Newberry Co., SC → Lauderdale Co., TN
FACT mother of J. C. Waits per Find a Grave (via William) · HYPOTHESIS (strong) Earhart maiden name / 1850 household
Photo placeholder

Relation: Mother of James Clayborn Waits (linked on J. C.’s Find a Grave memorial #200803433); wife of James Samuel Waits. William’s 2nd-great-grandmother.

Name forms (FACT (index)): “Almcnia Earheart” (1850 index — a mis-transcription of Almenia); “Almena Waits” (1860); “Almma Waits” (1870); “A E Waits” (1900). Her granddaughter was named Mary Almenia Waits (b. 1899) (FACT, funeral record).

Timeline
