James Clayborn Waits

Apr 1864 – 20 Dec 1944
Biological line

Relation: Father of Carrie Love “Lovy” Waits Simpson; husband of Rhoda/Phobe Milburn (1st) and Gabie Pilkington Waits (2nd).

Own Find a Grave memorial #200803433 (do not use #200804333 — wrong person). Born Apr 1864 Newberry, SC; died 20 Dec 1944 Memphis, Shelby Co., TN; buried Maplewood Cemetery, Ripley, Lauderdale Co., TN (stone: James C. Waits 1864–1944, next to parents). Parents: James Samuel Waits (1830–1910) and Almenia Earhart Waits (1831–1908), both Maplewood. Spouse linked: Gabie Pilkington Waits. Carrie Love FaG bio said birth year 1863 — prefer stone/FaG Apr 1864 and note the Carrie bio variant. Maley and Garner name him father of Carrie Love Simpson, Virginia Elleene Chaney, and Mary Almenuia Williams.
