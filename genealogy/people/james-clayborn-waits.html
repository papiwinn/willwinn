James Clayborn Waits

Apr 1864 – 20 Dec 1944
Biological line
Photo placeholder

Relation: Father of Carrie Love “Lovy” Waits Simpson; husband of Rhoda Milburn (1st) and Gabie Pilkington Waits (2nd).

Own Find a Grave memorial #200803433 (do not use #200804333 — wrong person). Born Apr 1864 Newberry, SC; died 20 Dec 1944 Memphis, Shelby Co., TN; buried Maplewood Cemetery, Ripley, Lauderdale Co., TN (stone: James C. Waits 1864–1944, next to parents). Parents on Find a Grave: James Samuel Waits (1830–1910) and Almenia Earhart Waits (1831–1908), both Maplewood. Carrie Love FaG bio said birth year 1863 — prefer stone/FaG Apr 1864 and note the Carrie bio variant. Maley and Garner name him father of Carrie Love Simpson, Virginia Elleene Chaney, and Mary Almenuia Williams.

FACT (memoir) Albert F. Parker’s 1978 memoir calls him “Mr. Claiborne (J.C.) Waits”, an old friend of Parker’s father; on 7 Jul 1918 the two rode over the Fort Pillow battlefield recalling “days when they were boys” and their work at Col. J. E. Walker’s gin and sawmill. HYPOTHESIS This implies the family came from South Carolina to Lauderdale in the 1870s.
