Austin Hunley Winn

27 Feb 1921 – 16 Nov 1996
FACT Father’s side · letters/manuscript subject
Photo placeholder

Relation: William’s father. Subject of the WWII letters and manuscript on willwinn.xyz. Spouse: Mary Oletta Littleheart Simpson Winn (b. 13 Feb 1924) — do not list her as Austin’s mother.

FACT Parents (William): father Willis Hunley Winn; mother Fannie Lee (McBride) Winn (b. 1894 Tipton). Letters address father as Mr. W. H. Winn / Mr & Mrs W. H. Winn, Route 2, Henning (d. Mar 1943). Parker 1978 memoir corroborates Willis & Fannie and sister Mrs. John Peyton (secondary).

FACT Born 27 February 1921 (William; matches grave marker Austin_s Grave.jpg). Death 16 November 1996; 1st Lt US Army Air Corps, WWII (marker). FACT Buried in Ripley Cemetery, Ripley, Lauderdale Co., TN, on a double stone with Oletta.
