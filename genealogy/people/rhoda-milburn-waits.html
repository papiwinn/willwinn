Rhoda Milburn Waits

c. 1873 – 14 May 1908 · aka Phobe / Rosa / Rhonda
Biological line · FACT marriage 1891 · HYPOTHESIS (strong) parents Mack & Mary Milburn
Photo placeholder

Relation: First wife of James Clayborn Waits; biological mother of Carrie Love “Lovy” Waits Simpson and her sisters.

Name: FACT “Rhoda” on her own 1891 marriage licence (the primary record) and in Carrie’s FaG bio — the best-supported form. Her children’s funeral records give variants: “Phobe” (Carrie, Maley), “Rosa” (Elleene) and “Rhonda” (Mary). HYPOTHESIS The 1900 index gives her initials “R E”, so her middle initial was probably E.

→ James Clayborn Waits · → Lovy · → Mack Milburn (probable father) · → Mary Owens Milburn (probable mother)
