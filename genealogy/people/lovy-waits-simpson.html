Carrie Love “Lovy” Waits Simpson

11 Jul 1892 – 26 Aug 1985
Biological line
Photo placeholder

→ Elleene Chaney · → Mary Williams · → Giles Rexford Simpson · → Mary Oletta Littleheart Simpson Winn

Relation: Daughter of James C. Waits and Phobe (Rhoda) Milburn; stepdaughter of Gabie Pilkington Waits; widow of Giles Rexford Simpson (Maley FH: “Prexford”). William’s grandmother line (Waits → Simpson).

Funeral-home and family sources name Carrie Love (Lovy/Lovey) Waits Simpson as daughter of James C. Waits and Phobe Milburn, later with stepmother Mrs. Gaby/Gabie Waits. She was the widow of Giles Rexford Simpson (FaG spouse link; Maley FH spells “Prexford”). Find a Grave #55032391. Linked children include Oletta Simpson Winn (1924–2017).
