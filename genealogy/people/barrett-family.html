The line

  • FACT Robyn Louise Barrett — born 27 August 1958, Natick, Massachusetts; married William LaVaughan Winn 20 August 1983, Millis, Massachusetts (William). Birth also listed in the Millis 1958 town report.
  • FACT Robyn’s siblings (William, family record, 27 Sep 2026): sisters Kathryn A. Barrett, Bonny Jane Barrett, Gayle Elizabeth Barrett; brothers Richard Clinton Barrett Jr., Peter Barrett, Paul Barrett, Mark Barrett. Living — names only.
  • FACT Father — Richard Clinton Barrett: born 26 January 1923 (William: Islington (Westwood), MA; DISCREPANCY birth registered in Norwood); died 29 April 1996 (William). Millis Superintendent of Streets / Public Works 1960–76, Fire Department Captain 1961–68
  • FACT Mother — Elizabeth Jane “Betty” Holden: born 2 August 1923, Milford, MA (William; the 1950 census gives Massachusetts); died 3 November 2015, Millis, MA, aged 92 (William; Millis 2015 report)
  • FACT (image) Married: 13 October 1945, Medway, MA — Millis 1945 report, “Marriages recorded in Millis,” p.31: Richard C. Barrett of Millis and Elizabeth J. Holden of Medway; Rev. Warren G. Hunt officiated
  • FACT 1950 census: Richard and Elizabeth in Millis-Clicquot, Norfolk Co., MA — Richard C (27, fireman at the State Hospital; his mother born in Nova Scotia), and Elizabeth J (26, born Massachusetts)
  • FACT Richard’s parents — Royden Y. Barrett and Lillian (Garland) Barrett: named on Richard’s 1923 birth record. Royden died 12 May 1939, Millis; Lillian died 15 Nov 1969, Wrentham. HYPOTHESIS (strong) that these death records are the same Royden and Lillian.
  • HYPOTHESIS Royden’s father — Henry Waldo Barrett (c.1843–1902, Norwood): circumstantial only; no record says “son of.” Royden’s mother: unknown.
  • NOT FOUND Lillian’s parents — HYPOTHESIS Frank W. Garland of Norwood may have been her brother
  • FACT Elizabeth’s parents — William M. Holden and Agnes Corbett: William M. Holden lived in Blackstone, Massachusetts; Agnes was born in Eastport, Maine (William). Their parents: unknown — research open
