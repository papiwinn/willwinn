Research lead (not on the tree)

  • HYPOTHESIS Research lead only: Simon Peter Driver (b. 1842), whose 1930 death abstract gives a Winn mother, was probably a close Winn relative — on this reading, a grandson of Peter Winn. The likely link is that Driver’s mother was one of Peter Winn’s daughters, Louisa J. (b. 1813) or Mary (1815–1880) (FS tree). That would make Driver a nephew of Willis’s father, William Hunley Winn, and so Willis’s first cousin, born 33 years before him. It would explain W. P. Winn being the informant on the certificate, and the family’s use of the name Peter (Peter Winn; Simon Peter Driver; William Peter Winn). In 1920 Peter Driver, 77, lived in the Winn household at Cherry (FACT, 1920 census), which strengthens this hypothesis. Not proven and not on the tree; the certificate image and Driver’s 1930 obituary would test it. See Willis’s page for the Driver evidence (Munford Cemetery stone, Fort Pillow placard, “Uncle Peter” in Austin’s letters).
