Tuesday May 1, 1945

Dear Mother And Sis,

A pretty day here today. Glad to get your letters yesterday. And also I got two copies of the Lauderdale County Enterprise. I've been busy reading them this morning. Went to a medical lecture earlier today. Expect to fly this afternoon. Test Hop. 

It's 9 AM now. We usually get our mail around 10:30 AM and 4:30 PM. Lots of packages the last few days but still no sheets as yet. What's the latest from Jack?

Maby you saw the write-up in the paper a few days ago about the B-29, The City of Memphis!! It's here on this field and Lt. Handwerker is the Airplane Commander. He is from Memphis. The paper kinda mis-represented the facts and all it talked about was some Colonel from Memphis who had nothing to do with that plane -- or with naming it.

I wrote Otho a letter last night. first time I've written him in a long time. Is he still working on the boat?

Later Tuesday nite:
Well I've been up flying this afternoon - Had a nice time - We took about 4 C-Bs up for a ride. On the first take-off we had a plug come out of one engine & lost about 40 gallons of oil so had to land again & stop up the hole & refill with oil. Then we took off again and broke in the new engine I wrote about earlier. 

Glad to see the City of Memphis picture you sent. I wrote something about that earlier in this letter but it was another article I spoke of.

Received the last pictures of John Winn Friday. They are sure good. I'll send some back to you. Also got the watermelon seed from both you & Mary F. thanks The garden looks swell. We already have watermelons with 4 leaves on them tho. Radishes about 1 in". The first flowers I planted are sure slow almost coming up. We have at least 10 flower packets of seed now. The other fellows have been getting seed also.

Better go now,
Love Austin

Dearest Folks.

May 5th 11 A.M. Saturday
10 Missions

Hello again! I started to write this letter Thursday nite before I left on the last mission but didn't get time to write. I got to bed about 2 AM this morning & slept till 10AM. Feel OK now & pretty rested up. The showers had all the water turned off so I went back to the helmet again to get cleaned up. I got  the Watermelon & Musk melon seed you sent, Thanks.

We bombed the Japanese airfields again. We were attacked by a few fighters but they weren't too sharp. They did kinda give us a surprise on their first attack.

The news sounded grand. We listened to the news on the way to Japan and heard of Hitler's and Goebels' death & also of the surrender of the German army in Italy & Southern Austria. Hope the peace conference is doing a good job.

Oh, guess who I saw Thursday! I was lying in my sack dozing off before the mission & A.V. Winn comes in it woke me up. Sure was glad to see him. He's the kid from Walla Walla Washington.

He is in B-24 & has 7 missions in. That makes all 3 of the Winns on this island now. (Ha) He's only temporarily here tho.

I took him down & showed him our B-29. He was really impressed over such a big plane.

Well it's time for the mail now so I'll rush this on over there and see if I've gotten any mail from you today.

Love to all;
Austin

May 5, 1945
Saturday

Dear Mother and Sis,
Wrote you a letter yesterday with the same date on it but was mistaken. Got mixed up on what day it was. Should have been Friday, the 4th instead.

It's raining here this morning. I just wrote Wilbur a letter. How is everyone around Cherry getting along? In the last seed you sent, I got some watermellon, muskmellon and I suppose the little round seed are turnip seed. Is that right?

What is John doing these days? Guess both tractors are kept pretty busy getting the corn land ready for planting.

It's time for mail now so I'll just send a note

Well so long,
Love to All,
Austin

Sunday, May 6th
Marianas

How's every one today,
Nice day here. We went to another airfield this morning & flew a 29 back to the home base. We had an awful bad rain last nite & as the planes were coming back from the mission, only one of our planes landed at this field. They are scattered throughout the Marianas and one other field on this island. We didn't go on this mission.

The boys are having a volleyball game this afternoon. Guess I'll have to go out and play with them. I got my first letter from Jack today. Glad to hear he's back in the states. He has to have another operation. Says he feels fine tho. 

Well I must go now. Tell all hello. Things will be hopping around here again soon. The missions will get rougher if we don't go to the Kyushu. We are glad to get those kind of missions. The strategic targets are better protected by the Japs.

Love to all,
Austin

May 8, 1945
Marianas

Dearest Mother & All,

Today is V-E day - Was sure glad to hear the news on the radio. Mr. Truman is supposed to talk tonight at 11PM Don't know if we will be awake to hear him or not. Today we bombed a Jap island for practice. Had a very nice trip & got back about 3PM. We had chicken which was very good.

Received a letter from Jack yesterday. Did you get the pictures I sent you? Think I sent 2.

Boy, Hughey really has some fancy articles in the Hi-Life, doesn't he!

The flowers around home must be blooming now. That is lots of them. I could see several in the picture you sent of John Winn.

We have our name painted on the plane now. Also a bomb for each mission & 3 Jap flags for 3 fighter planes our tail gunner shot down. Also each of our own names under our positions. The name of our plane is "City of Charlottesville" (Virginia)

Well I better get off to sleep now. Good night to all. 

Love & kisses,
Austin

May 9, 1945 
Marianas

Hello Mother & Sister,

Have been up bombing the islands again for practice. Wrote you a letter last nite but thought I'd drop you another little line this afternoon. Might not be where I can write tomorrow.

Heard the news this morning. Wasn't awake last nite to hear The President speak.

The mail hasn't come today as yet. Wasn't any morning mail for anyone. Still haven't received any of the packages. I have one coming from you, Rosie, Addie, and Dotty too I think.

How is the farming coming along? I've been trying to burn the little warts off my hands with nitric acid. Think I've gotten rid of a couple of them so far.

Has Miss Josie Belle come back from Florida yet? Hope Jack's operation is O.K.

Tell Grand pa hello & not to try to get around too much when he gets up.

Love to all
Austin

May 11 1945
Marianas


Dear folks,

How is everyone today? Got back from the mission on Japs oil refineries & storages last nite. There were over 400 B-29 up there. Boy, I've never seen so many planes in the air together. I landed on Iwo Jima on the return trip.

I haven't gotten my mail for yesterday yet. I'll go see if I had any then finish this letter. I believe I just gone up.  It's too late to eat breakfast. 8am. I ate a snack last night tho about 10 P.M.

Later-2 PM.
Will I received 2 letters from you today with several clippings included. I got the little memo books too thanks a lot. They are just the right size.

You said William Hutch might write to Lowry. Here's his address if you want to give it to him.


Col. Leon L. Lowry
Commanding Officer
93rd Bomb SQDN
APO 246 Unit 2 c/0 PM
San Francisco, Calif.

You've heard me speak of the Airplane Commander from Memphis and his plane. You sent me a picture of his plane. Well, he is coming home. He will send you something that I sent home by him. His name is St. John Handwerker. I hear Hilda Gardner is opening up her business again!! He said he might even drive up to our place one day.

Guess there will be more news in the paper about him when he gets to Memphis. Try to send me the clippings if you see them.

Well, I'll have to stop about now. Still cloudy & has been rather cool all day so far.

We really saw a lot of flak yesterday. Even the Jap ships in the harbor were shooting at us too. The target we were bombing really exploded. The flames & smoke came up over 10,000 ft high. The Jap fighters didn't bother us this time.

love to all
Austin

Love to all,
Austin

Friday Morning May 18

Nice morning, however it has been raining a good bit. I slept late this morning & didn't get any breakfast. 

I still have plenty of candy & cookies. In fact I sealed one can of cookies back up & put them away till we eat the others up.

Received the packets of flowers seed from you the other day. We have several flowers up but some have to be transplanted. The Phlox & Verbena didn't. We have Cox Combs, Old fashioned gardens, Zinnias, etc. Don't guess we'll need any more seed for a while. Our tomato patch looks good but there are some worms that are eating the leaves up. Radishes are big enough to eat.

Did you get the pictures? Well I'll say Bye now. 

Love to all,
Austin

Glad to hear Jack is getting along OK. I haven't used the sheets yet.(HA) Kinda gotten used to sleeping without them. I will tho.

14 Missions 236 Hours
May 21, 1945
Marianas

Dear Mother & Sis,

Didn't get to write to you yesterday. We've been pretty busy lately. We flew 3 times to Japan last week then yesterday we bombed a Jap Island for practice.

Our engines were getting pretty old. On our last mission we had an oil plug drop out while we were right over Tokyo & lost all the oil out of that engine - We feathered it & bombed the city of Hamamatsu & flew back to Iwo Jima on 3 engines. Landed there & got the engine fixed & refilled with oil. We flew on back home that nite. We are getting 3 new engines on our plane now before we fly it again. You know we put one new one on it a coupla weeks ago. 

I was awarded the "Air Medal" for the flight over Kobe when we stayed over the target 2 hours.

I went over this morning & signed up to remain in the Army until Japan is defeated. Not that I would have been able to get out anyway I guess. But we were supposed to put a choice down.

I will get an increase in pay this month of about $25. I was the only pilot in my class (44-D) to make 1st Lt. Another guy's promotion came through but he was lost over Tokyo several weeks ago.

Well I've got to go out & play ball with the fellows now. I'll probably miss the next coupla missions while our engines are being changed.

Have you heard from Lt. Handwerker yet? He is supposed to send you my pictures.
Tell all hello.
Love & Kisses
Austin

May 24, 1945
Thursday nite

Dear Sis & Mother,

Today has been a very pleasant one. If Jack has written lately how is he? Thought I'd just drop a line or two tonite before going to bed. Know I won't get to write you again for a coupla days. Maybe three so don't worry any if you don't hear from me for several days this time.

Today I went down to the beach & went swimming. Went to another Airfield where A.V. was but didn't see him anywhere. I ate supper there & we had beef steak which was very good. Also drank a coupla cold Coca-Colas while there which tasted swell. Received your letter today. They come very regularly. Sometimes they come by two's tho.

Well, I'll say nity nite about now. What's Big John doing these days? Tell him hello for me. 

Love to all,
Austin

Monday May 29, 1945

Dear Mother & Sister

Well I'm back again after several days of touring the Pacific & not writing to you. I got back today & fouund about 4 letters from you waiting for me. Was really glad to get them. I've been gone since last Friday & didn't shave unmtil I got bac today. Boy I sure feel better now. I spent 5 nites on Iwo Jima and one on Tinian. 

Was really glad to hear Jack has been home. Guess he's stationed at Millington now!  Was La Vaughan there at the same time as Jack? A surprise to me that Jack is up walking around & going to Ripley!  Maybe he will get out of the service as soon as he is well again. 

I'll send the pictures of the kids back. They are sure cute. I sthe little boy Clifford Jr.?

We were on a special type duty this last trip.(HA) We thought we would never get back from Iwo. We were hitch-hikers. We brought a B-29 back to our first stop that had been repaired at Iwo then we hooked a C47 back to base. And a good shower. Well I'll get this off. 

Request: Please send me some stationery. 

Love,
Austin
