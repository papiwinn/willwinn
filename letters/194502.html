Officers" Club Kearney Army Air Field Kearney, Nebr. Feb. 2, 1945 Dear Mother, Another busy day and it's still so cold too. Snow on the ground and still looks like more. How has everyone been? I haven't been able to get any mail since I left G.B. I sure hope this mail situation of mine doesn't get too mixed up. You see I was in the 93rd & am now 28th but will be 93rd again soon. I will send you a temporary APO No. soon. Boy you can call me "Shorty" now. I got a hair cut a few minutes ago & it's at least 1/4 inch long. I think. I had about 3 rolls of film in the studio in G.B. being developed. I had to leave them there but I asked Rosie to get them for me & send them if possible. If she doesn't send them soon I'll send her your address & let her dend them to you. I got a telephone call from Evelyn last week & a telegram the day I left. I'm living in the same B.O.Q with the commanding Officer & His staff. I'm the only officer lower than a Major in this building. Of course I have a Majors job. now also tho. I'm Acting Assist. Group Operations Officer until I get back with my crew. It was really a streak of luck that I got to come along with these guys. Just as I was about to give up One pilot up here broke his leg so Major Maley the Assist. Operations Officer had to take over this pilot's crew. So as I was right there trying to get across with this group the just made me the Assist. Grp Operat. Officer. Say I got paid just before leaving & I didn't have much time to tend to my affairs so I just bought 3 war bonds & left the money & your adress etc. & told them to mail them to you. If you haven't recieved themas yet I'd like to know so I could get it strait if possible. I left the money there without a receipt or anything. I am taking out an allotment to send home 150 each month and I want you to buy bonds for me as it comes in. I could send the bonds home but that is a lot more complicated affair after you get over seas. You can just let John take the check into the bank & get 2 $100 bonds each month. The Russians are really doing OK it seems. Oh yes. I found my other fountain pen since I've been here. It was in a shirt in my laundry bag. Also the one foot locker I sent had some dirty clothes in it but they are good clothes. The fellow just threw them away & bought new ones. I saw all the fellows on my old crew & they were all glad to see me too. Binder\s (navigator) father died tho & he had to go on an emergency leave. Don't know if he will get back in time or not. Well I hope everything is just fine around home. Now don't you be worrying about me. I'll write as often as possible but maybe there will be times that you wont hear for quite a while. But you can understand that. I'm sleeping in the same room with Major Lowery who use to be stationed at Dyersburg. He says that he knows William Hutcherson & took him for a plane ride once. He said tell him hello if you see him. Well tell Sis, John and John Winn hi also. Hope grandpa feels better & likes the cigars. Love to all Austin

Officers' Club Kearney Army Air Field Kearney, Nebr. Feb. 5, 1945 Dear Mother, How's everything?. Well I got some mail today for the first time since I left Great Bend. A couple letters from you & sister. Here is a picture taken while I was home. One day while in Ripley Mr. Claude Robbins girl saw me & wanted to take my pictIn the court yard. I should get another in a few days from another girl that was with her (HA). Everythings going swell here except the weather is so bad we can't fly. Just after we stopped here it started snowing & has been bad ever since. I have about 3 rolls of film being developed at Great Bend. I wrote for Rosie to send to me here if possible. But if she doesn't get them to me I'll send her your address. Don't worry tho I don't think she'll write you too many letters (HA). I got 2 letters from Evelyn & she said she was writing you another letter. She is a nice sweet girl alright but she must have a wrong idea about how I feel about her! Well everyone is having a swell time around here. Knowing they wont be back for quite a while. How's John doing with the farming? I have got to eat supper now. Hope everythings fine there. I'm holding a Major's job now temporarily. After I'm over I'll go back with my same crew. I'll have to send my wool clothes home when I leave here. You can have them cleaned & put away.

Officers' Club Kearney Army Air Field Kearney, Nebr. Dear Mother, I got your letter today and am sorry Evelyn is causing you so much worry. I don't know what she means by doing that way. You can tell her anything you like. I wouldn't want her coming to see you & causing you all that much trouble. the way she writes to me she talks like we're engaged and it's just a matter of time till we'll be married.(HA) I don't care to be engaged just yet & if you know the words to use to slow her up a bit well go right ahead. She called me on the phone one day & the connection was so bad I could hardly hear her. I mentioned grandpa was in bed so she must have thought I said DEAD. I guess I'll have to write her a letter & tell her to lay OFF. You probably wont here from me for several days now. This is a pretty day. The sun is shining pretty but it's still pretty cold. I'm with Cook & tge other guys every day but am not flying the same plane. Expect to after a while tho. I really was lucky to get this break after the way things turned out just before leaving Great Bend. I'm going over in style occupying a Majors position & job. Has been pretty good since I've been around here. I've had much more time than the other boys. During all that snow they were getting up before daylight every morning & I've just slept until I automatically wake up. About 9AM. (HA) Rosie mailed the pictures to me but I haven't received them yet. I told some of the other fellows to get my mail for me if I left begore them. I think I sent the negatives of those pictures you asked about home in one of the footlockers. Now here's a list of stuff I've sent home recently so you can check up & see if you have it all. Jan 31st: 3-$100 War Bonds from Great Bend 3-Footlockers 1-Barracks Bag from here 1-Box cigars " " 1-Box candy " " My gold top fountain pen is in a green shirt pocket in the Barracks Bag. the letter I got today from you came direct to the base. You can send all my mail through this base now until you get a newer address. Great Bend is so slow about forwarding any mail from there Well Mother, I don't know much else to write. will make one more stop in U.S. So be good & take care of things as best you can till I get back. Was glad to hear that John Winn has that tooth. He'll probably have some more soon. I'll be writing again soon as possible. Love to all Austin

AMERICAN RED CROSS Thursday Feb. 8, 1945 Hello Mother & All, Well I'm in a different part of the country today. I'm at a Port of Embarcation in California. Every thing is just fine. I sent my laundry out tonite & will get it back tomorrow. Didn't have any stationery with me so I picked up this Res Cross stationery at the B.O.Q. Hdqs. Hope all is fine around home. This adress will be my temporary APO#. You can use that now until I send you a later change of address. When you get my last & permanent A.P.O.# with a card it entitles you to send one package to me. Don't know of anything I'll need tho. After that the only thing you can send will be something I write & ask for. I ran into an old buddie from Roswell here tonite. I started to call you last nite from Kearney but you had to wait 2 or 3 hours to ge a call through. I'll be writing again in a few days. We are pretty busy here but are well taken care of. I have my camera with me but have to have it sealed up until I get across. Also this wrist watch has Swiss Movement & I have to register that also so I can bring it back when we come into the states again. I think I'll get a new watch issued to me also. I have a thousand dollars worth of equipment issued to me now I guess. Not counting a plane ect. I guess I'd better be getting to bed now so sweet dreams to you all. It's just 9PM here but it's 11PM where you are & I got up this morning on your time.(HA) Hope the neighbors have heard from Bill Waits & Jack lately. The way things are going now I'm going to beat Cook & the others overseas. Well solong for now Love to all Austin Lt. Austin H. Winn, 0.774857 19th Bomb Group APO# 18179-CZ-3 c/o Post Master San Francisco, Calif.

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES Monday Feby. 12th 6:30 PM Dear Mother and Sis, How is everyone today? Hope you're doing OK there. I just took a little nap & am about ready to eat supper. I didn't get any sleep all last night to amount to anything. I left Cook behind again but expect to see him again tomorrow. Boy, we are hearing some good music now. Sure is hot I don't need any wool clothing any more for a while. I haven't heard from you since I left Kearney. This is going to have to come to a stop one of these days.(HA) What is John Winn doing now? Has he cut any new teeth? I just heard the news on a loud speaker about the meeting of the Big Three And their plans for Germany after the war. Boy I'm just beginning to realize what a big old world this is. And so much water! If I get into Town Tomorrow I'll try to send you a souvenir of some kind. I won't do like Paul Sales tho & get left behind.(HA) I'd sure like to run into him as Waits. Well for that matter any of the guys I know. There are several persons from around Ripley working here anyway. Well I see the rest of the fellows are about ready to eat. They are a swell gang too. We have a lot of fun joking about our different mishaps & how everything turns out ok anyway. Well Solong for tonite. Tell the Sides & Simps hello when you see them & hope they have heard from Bill & Jack. For once & still have the same address.(HA) APO 18179-CZ-3 % Postmaster Love to All, Austin

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES Somewhere in the Marianas Feb. 16, 1945 Dear Mother and All, Well I have finally reached my destination. Got here yesterday but didn't have time to write. We had a very interesting trip over & everything went O-KAY. Cook isn't here yet until I want to see the C.O. & he said I would get back on that crew. The fellows I rode over with are trying to keep me in their squad. now - Didn't know I was so impt.(HA) This is really out-door life. We live in tents & sleep on cots with nothing but one blanket. No sheets.(HA) Shave, Bathe, Wash our cloths out of a steel helmet. We have a nice runway but the biovac area was jungle 3 or 4 days ago. We don't have any showers or places to shave yet. We are going to build all this up tho & it will be pretty nice when it's finished. We cannot take any pictures around here at all. Maybe sometime later tho. Everyone is busy & I have a lot to do today. I want to move back our with my buddies. Tell John this place looks something like that clearing down in the Miss. Bottoms where we hauled so much wood from except the dirt is red & its covered with tents. The jungles are so thick I don't see how anyone can even walk through them. We aren't allowed to leave the camp clearing. They will cost material you if you go into the jungles because there are still Japs around. And Marine patrols that shoot anything they see. Well as soon as I get more time I'll write you some more so tell everyone hello. I'm in the 21st Bomber Command. We are at a brand new field just hacked out of the jungle. The food is swell. Love to All, Austin Lt Austin H Winn O-774857 19th Bomb Group, 93rd Sqdn. A.P.O # 246 , Unit 2 c/o Postmaster San Francisco, Calif. Don't forget to put Unit 2 because that distinguishes which part of the island I'm on. I brought this stationery from Calif. The envelope from Honolulu. I didn't have time to write that letter & told Oletta I would write you.

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES Mariana Islands, Feb. 18, 1945 Hello Sister of all, How have you all been? Guess you haven't heard from me in several days! I wrote Mother a letter yesterday. Well it rained again last nite and again this morning. Makes it kinda muddy around here too. I moved over with the 93rd Sgd. last nite. I'm still assigned to the 28th & they are trying to keep me but I went to the C.O. & he told me to move our here. If Cook would come on in here I could get transferred back on that crew. He should be here tomorrow anyway. I just went out I shaved out of a helmet. We eat in mess kits right out under the sky. All this is being worked on tho & before long we'll have things better. The crews are already bombing some of their islands around here. We will be in a day or two We sleep under mosquito bars but they aren't too bad (the thing I need is a couple of sheets). If you get time and know how to go about sending them well send me some sheets. There is some rule they told us about that you can send one package when you got my APO#. After that I have to request what I want. The Postmaster in Ripley or Henning should know. Later in the day now the sun will come out & it will be HOT. I'm just wearing a 1 piece suit now - my blue swim trunks. Am trying to get used to this sun without blistering. I understand it takes about 7 days for you to get my mail & about 2 weeks for me to get yours. I haven't received any yet but maybe soon (HA) Well how's that Johnie Kake & his teeth coming along? Everyone around here is constructing little tables & desks to write on. And trying to get their tent comfortable Well Sis I think I'll have to go now I want to build a little wash stand. I have a .45 automatic pistol, 10 inch knife and a lot of extra gear while I'm flying. We hear the news every day & also have an open air theatre. I'm feeling fine & think this open air life will be good for me. The only trouble is it's so damp & hard to keep down fungus diseases like athlete foot. So long now Love to All Austin Will be glad when I get all that mail I've been missing.

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES Feby. 18, 1945 Sunday 6 PM Dear Mother, The sun is about 20 minutes high. Guess I'll have time to write a few lines before it gets too dark. I'm feeling swell. Just went down to a rig they have fixed up & took a good shower. A truck comes by & sprays everyone with a hose at about 5:30 each afternoon so that's quite an improvement. How is everyone in Cherry? Soon as my crew gets here let be going on some bombing raids. We have certain things that we can't write about. I'll try to tell you as much as possible but we have to go by censorship regulations. Let me know if a censor marks anything out of my letters! Well, you can ask me anything you like & I'll answer them if possible Better close down now as it's getting too dark. I'll Write some more tomorrow.

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES Feb 19, 1945 Monday Well everything is hunky dory this morning! Got up this morning and it wasn't raining. That is something around here and we're in the dry season right now... The wet season begins around April or May. Then it rains about 25 days out of a month. They have opened up a little PX. now where you can get stationery, soap, candy, and toilet articles. Our trip across was very nice. I was at the Port of Embarcation in California for about 3 days. Then we flew to Hawaii. I saw Pearl Harbor, Honolulu, Hickam Field & John Rodgers field. I'm not allowed to tell of our trip from there on to destination. It was sure plenty a water to cross tho. I'm around 7,000 miles from home I guess now. When I get over Japan that well be another 1500 or better to add. LATER: I just ate lunch and we had Salmon Cakes - Beans, rice, sliced pineapple, bread Coffee etc. NOT BAD AT ALL. We hear music most all day long over the Armed Forces Radio which is broadcast over a loud speaker that can be heard all over the camp. Well I've got to go down to the orderly room & get my financial matters straightened out. You may not get a check from the government for several weeks yet how ever when I get paid at the end of the month I'll probably send some money home if there is any available means. I don't see where I can use any money out here except maybe a few dollars a month for candy & other little items. Our meals cost 75 cents a day. LATER: Sameday. Still waiting around for my boys to come in. Meanwhile I'm getting a little of this sun every once in a while. My face is plenty red but not burned. My shoulders are getting a little sore tho. I don't think Jack is on the same island as I. Does his folks know where he is yet? I sent you a letter with a dollar bill in it marked "Hawaii". Have you gotten it yet? I just wondered if the censor took it out or held up the letter for a while or not. I wrote to Wither & Jack already. Lee write Waits & Carl soon. Looks like I'm going to be plenty busy around here soon. I may not be able to write as often as I have been. I'll write every chance tho so don't worry. You notice I changed my address back to the 93rd Sgdn because I'm pretty sure I'll get back with them. Right now I'm assigned to the 28th and I live with the 93rd.(HA) I just go back & forth from one Sgdn to the other. Do just about as I please. Soon as my crew gets here tho I'll be busy flying or going to school! We had a man from Siapan give us a lecture this morning. Think he put on my knife and go out scouting around. I see some new faces around. Think I better go see who's here. Tell all hello and everythings OK over here. Love Austin

Feby 21, 1945 In The Marianas 4 P.M. Dear Mother and all, Well I got my first letters today. And guess who it was from? The little Robbins girl sent me the other picture she took of me that day in Ripley. Don't know why this letter beat all others here. She sent it Air Mail Feb 8th. But it went to Kearney & then to San Francisco & so on. Everything is going swell out here. I'm still in the same place and will be until they get our little Quonset Huts built. They are made out of tin & are just half of a circle. Looks something like this:(hand drawn picture here) Only the combat crews get to live in these kind of buildings Everyone else will remain in tents. The weather has been pretty nice today. It's always cool enough to sleep good at nite here. Yesterday another guy & myself went down to the south end of the island in a Jeep. We ran across some other military installations & I managed to get a mattress cover. This will be good to sleep on. How is everything around home? Is grandpa feeling any better lately? (Same letter) Feby 22, 1945 Well today is Washington's Birthday Yesterday was Otho's. By the way how is Otho? Is he still down with his dad & Jr. I guess I'll have to write him a letter also one of these days. Its raining this morning and this paper is so soft & damp. I have a job this morning censoring the enlisted mens mail. Maybe I'll get a few pointers on how to write letters now. Cook will surely be here today. Our bombardier got sick in the States at POE and they had to leave him there in the hospital. That is the reason they were so late. Our POE (Port of Embarkation) was Mather Field Calif. Right close to Sacramento. Right close to Sacramento. It's almost noon now & I've been busy reading letters all morning. Boy you sure can learn just how everyone is getting along & how everyones morale is by censoring the mail. Not many of them try to write anything that they shouldn't. It's funny to see how some try to tell where they are in a round about way. The $25 I put into the Officers Club back in the States is coming in handy now. We have 6 washing machines about like yours & I'm getting my clothes washed this morning in one of them. We also bought a nice Radio & Record player with that money & it plays over a speaker all day & till about 10 o'clock at nite. The special services part of army furnish a movie projector & we have a free movie every nite. Right out in the open. A screen tacked up on a tree & everyone sits on the ground or brings a box or chair of some kind with them.(HA) Last nite we saw "My Favorite Wife" a good picture too & the nite before "Going My Way". That made the 3rd time I'd seen that one. Everyone goes to the movies tho whether they've seen them or not. We have no lights in our tents anyway except flashlights so we might as well see them. I guess I better close for now & read a few more letters. Then go check the mail to see if I've received any mail myself from the States. Am standing this sun O.K. & the cool breezes blow most all the time. Its a swell place. So long as it isn't raining. Solong Buddie

Feby. 23, 1945 In the Marianas 10 A.M. Dear Mother & All, Will begin this letter now although I may not get it finished today. Everything is swell here except Cook hasn't gotten here yet. He started on his last lap yesterday but understand he had an engine failure & I had to return to his take-off field. He may be several days getting here now if he has to have the engine worked on. While I'm waiting for him I'll have a job censoring the mail & doing lots of odd jobs around here. OH! A big formation of 29's just came over the field. They sure look pretty. We are missing a lot of flying & bombing by him being so long coming. Yesterday I got a wad of mail. But none from you all yet. I understand that, so don't think I'm getting restless about my mail.(HA) I heard from Geraldine C. & she said John B. was a prisoner of Germany. Was sure glad to hear that. I got my washing done yesterday and it's on the line this morning. I guess sister has been guessing my whereabouts, has she not? There is nothing in this letter to point to my whereabouts so tell her not to try to read between the lines.(HA) Here's the way I have figured it out. It's all quiet confusing but when its: 6AM where you are its 4PM same day here. 12 Noon " " " " 10 PM " " ". 6 PM where you are its 2 AM next day here. On our way across we crossed the International Date line. We took off on Wednesday morning & landed Thursday afternoon according to the calendar & time.(HA) Altho' we were in the air only 8 hours. Guess I'll have to stop now for a while. I'll be back tho' so don't go away. LATER: 12:30 (noon) Well I got a letter from you and sister just now. Sure am glad to see that we've made connection. Yours was written Feb 15th so I still have lots of older ones yet to come. I'm not supposed to write on the back of this paper I don't think. The censors don't have time to look around on the back for writing. This stationery is so thick & heavy over 2 pages makes an Air Mail letter pretty heavy. Tell Miss Josie Belle to write Jack that I'll be seeing him soon, I hope. For him to watch for any B-29s landing there. I hope she has heard from Jack lately. I'm sitting out in the shade under a tree now. Brought a cot & my table outside where I could get the cool breeze. Guess I'll sign off pretty soon. I better write several letters today while I have the time. Hope John is over that sore throat. That was too much dock hunting NO DOUBT! Am glad Willard got to come home. Our base is improving every day. We should have a mess hall in a few days. And better living quarters also. There were three Gobs(?) here today. They had beards a year old it looked like. Don't know where they came from. Love to all, Austin

Feb 25, 1945 Somewhere in The Marianas Sunday Dear Mother & Sis, Well, I got your second letter yesterday. Guess they will begin coming kinda regular now. This took only 8 days. Also Cook came in yesterday so I'm back with him and in a new tent. We've gonna be busy now! Guess you'll see where some of the guys are busy today! Sure was glad to hear from you again. We had chicken for dinner today. Can you believe that! Has anyone heard from Jack lately? Write & let me know if that is true about John Bishop being a prisoner of war! This morning we were all hot from setting up the tent & digging a ditch around it. It came a good shower & I ran out in the rain & got all soaped up & it stopped raining & then I had to get my helmet & get some water to get the soap off (HA) A nice breeze blowing today. I went down to fly last nite. We had a little difficulty tho it didn't take off. We had two soldiers from another branch of the service going to ride along with us for their first airplane ride. They were disappointed. The generals on this island didn't like the idea of us not wearing a complete uniform so I guess I won't be wearing the swim suit so much now. We have to wear khaki shirts & trousers at all times except when taking physical training or manual labor. Most of the fellows are down looking the plane over now. Some already have their ship names & pictures printed on their planes. We haven't yet. LATER: Well it's Monday nite now. The 26th Have been on a little mission since I began the letter. Went to the show tonight & am censoring the mail now. Better say so long & get back to work. Love to all Austin

Feb 28, 1945 Marianas Dearest Mother, I didn't get time to write to you yesterday, my birthday. We were busy all day. Can't say as I feel any older today but just the same I'm 24 now & that is kinda OLD eh! I feel that I should try to express my gratitude to you for being such a wonderful mother. Thanks a million times for being so patient and kind to us while we were small & didn't understand things too clearly. And for raising us in a Christian home. You're just too sweet for words and I love you. Things around here are still on the improving side. And one day I got 15 letters. About half them from you & sister. They were sure lots of pleasure to read. Was glad to get caught up on all this back news that I'd missed. Sure was proud to hear about John B. I also got some of the pictures from Rosie that I took down in Puerto Rico. I guess she will send you the negatives soon. I think she was having done reprints made from some of them. I used Cooks Type Writer to address the envelope. We went on a mission the other day. Our planes were in on the Big raid on Tokyo the (redacted). None of our planes were damaged & all returned safely. Some were flying for 18 hours tho' I were really tired out when they returned. We weren't in on that one tho'. Cook didn't get here soon enough. The Navy also hit Tokyo the same day. That helped out a lot as the Jap planes couldn't get up to our planes on account of such bad weather. Tell sister she only use the second when writing to her - How is John Winn's cold getting along? Hope he is over with it by now. How's Grandpa also? Am glad you found something you could use in the foot-lockers. Oh my radio Operator & I went out & climbed a Coconut tree got 10 nice Coconuts. We ate & drank the milk from one. It sure is good. We'll be careful not to get sick on them tho so don't worry.(HA). When you get the negatives, have about 3 extra pictures of me with the stick in my hand down by the beach made & send them to me. I have had several request for pictures lately.(HA) This is about all the time I've got now. Hope they've heard from Jack by now. I write to these guys out here I put "Inter Island" on my envelope & it's suppose to go to them without first returning to San Francisco then back. As yet tho I haven't had an answer. Wither has 11 missions in now & has moved to the Philippines. Tell all hello. Love, Austin
