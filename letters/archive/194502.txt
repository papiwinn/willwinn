Feb. 2, 1945

Dear Mother,

Another busy day and it's still so cold too. Snow on the ground and still looks like more.

How has everyone been? I haven't been able to get any mail since I left G.B. I sure hope this mail situation of mine doesn't get too mixed up. You see I was in the 93rd & am now 28th but will be 93rd again soon. I will send you a temporary APO No. soon.

Boy you can call me "Shorty" now. I got a hair cut a few minutes ago & it's at least 1/4 inch long. I think. I had about 3 rolls of film in the studio in G.B. being developed. I had to leave them there but I asked Rosie to get them for me & send them if possible. If she doesn't send them soon I'll send her your address & let her dend them to you.

I got a telephone call from Evelyn last week & a telegram the day I left.

I'm living in the same B.O.Q with the commanding Officer & His staff. I'm the only officer lower than a Major in this building. Of course I have a Majors job. now also tho. I'm Acting Assist. Group Operations Officer until I get back with my crew.

It was really a streak of luck that I got to come along with these guys. Just as I was about to give up One pilot up here broke his leg so Major Maley the Assist. Operations Officer had to take over this pilot's crew. So as I was right there trying to get across with this group the just made me the Assist. Grp Operat. Officer.

Say I got paid just before leaving & I didn't have much time to tend to my affairs so I just bought 3 war bonds & left the money & your adress etc. & told them to mail them to you.

If you haven't recieved themas yet I'd like to know so I could get it strait if possible. I left the money there without a receipt or anything.

I am taking out an allotment to send home 150 each month and I want you to buy bonds for me as it comes in. I could send the bonds home but that is a lot more complicated affair after you get over seas. You can just let John take the check into the bank & get 2 $100 bonds each month.

The Russians are really doing OK it seems. Oh yes. I found my other fountain pen since I've been here. It was in a shirt in my laundry bag.

Also the one foot locker I sent had some dirty clothes in it but they are good clothes. The fellow just threw them away & bought new ones.

I saw all the fellows on my old crew & they were all glad to see me too. Binder\s (navigator) father died tho & he had to go on an emergency leave. Don't know if he will get back in time or not.

Well I hope everything is just fine around home. Now don't you be worrying about me. I'll write as often as possible but maybe there will be times that you wont hear for quite a while. But you can understand that.

I'm sleeping in the same room with Major Lowery who use to be stationed at Dyersburg. He says that he knows William Hutcherson & took him for a plane ride once. He said tell him hello if you see him.

Well tell Sis, John and John Winn hi also. Hope grandpa feels better & likes the cigars.

Love to all
Austin

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES

Somewhere in the Marianas
Feb. 16, 1945

Dear Mother and All,

Well I have finally reached my destination. Got here yesterday but didn't have time to write. We had a very interesting trip over & everything went O-KAY. Cook isn't here yet until I want to see the C.O. & he said I would get back on that crew. The fellows I rode over with are trying to keep me in their squad. now - Didn't know I was so impt.(HA)

This is really out-door life. We live in tents & sleep on cots with nothing but one blanket. No sheets.(HA) Shave, Bathe, Wash our cloths out of a steel helmet. We have a nice runway but the biovac area was jungle 3 or 4 days ago.

We don't have any showers or places to shave yet. We are going to build all this up tho & it will be pretty nice when it's finished.

We cannot take any pictures around here at all. Maybe sometime later tho.

Everyone is busy & I have a lot to do today. I want to move back our with my buddies.

Tell John this place looks something like that clearing down in the Miss. Bottoms where we hauled so much wood from except the dirt is red & its covered with tents. The jungles are so thick I don't see how anyone can even walk through them.

We aren't allowed to leave the camp clearing. They will cost material you if you go into the jungles because there are still Japs around. And Marine patrols that shoot anything they see.

Well as soon as I get more time I'll write you some more so tell everyone hello.

I'm in the 21st Bomber Command. We are at a brand new field just hacked out of the jungle. The food is swell.

Love to All,
Austin

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES

Mariana Islands,
Feb. 18, 1945

Hello Sister of all,

How have you all been? Guess you haven't heard from me in several days! I wrote Mother a letter yesterday.

Well it rained again last nite and again this morning. Makes it kinda muddy around here too. I moved over with the 93rd Sgd. last nite. I'm still assigned to the 28th & they are trying to keep me but I went to the C.O. & he told me to move our here. If Cook would come on in here I could get transferred back on that crew. He should be here tomorrow anyway. I just went out I shaved out of a helmet. We eat in mess kits right out under the sky. All this is being worked on tho & before long we'll have things better.

The crews are already bombing some of their islands around here. We will be in a day or two

We sleep under mosquito bars but they aren't too bad (the thing I need is a couple of sheets). If you get time and know how to go about sending them well send me some sheets. There is some rule they told us about that you can send one package when you got my APO#. After that I have to request what I want. The Postmaster in Ripley or Henning should know.

Later in the day now the sun will come out & it will be HOT. I'm just wearing a 1 piece suit now - my blue swim trunks. Am trying to get used to this sun without blistering.

I understand it takes about 7 days for you to get my mail & about 2 weeks for me to get yours. I haven't received any yet but maybe soon (HA)

Well how's that Johnie Kake & his teeth coming along? Everyone around here is constructing little tables & desks to write on. And trying to get their tent comfortable

Well Sis I think I'll have to go now   I want to build a little wash stand. I have a .45 automatic pistol, 10 inch knife and a lot of extra gear while I'm flying.

We hear the news every day & also have an open air theatre. I'm feeling fine & think this open air life will be good for me. The only trouble is it's so damp & hard to keep down fungus diseases like athlete foot.

So long now
Love to All
Austin

Will be glad when I get all that mail I've been missing.

UNITED STATES AIR FORCES

Feb 19, 1945
Monday

Well everything is hunky dory this morning! Got up this morning and it wasn't raining. That is something around here and we're in the dry season right now... The wet season begins around April or May. Then it rains about 25 days out of a month.

They have opened up a little PX. now where you can get stationery, soap, candy, and toilet articles.

Our trip across was very nice. I was at the Port of Embarcation in California for about 3 days. Then we flew to Hawaii. I saw Pearl Harbor, Honolulu, Hickam Field & John Rodgers field. I'm not allowed to tell of our trip from there on to destination. It was sure plenty a water to cross tho. I'm around 7,000 miles from home I guess now. When I get over Japan that well be another 1500 or better to add.

LATER:
I just ate lunch and we had Salmon Cakes - Beans, rice, sliced pineapple, bread Coffee etc. NOT BAD AT ALL. We hear music most all day long over the Armed Forces Radio which is broadcast over a loud speaker that can be heard all over the camp.

Well I've got to go down to the orderly room & get my financial matters straightened out. You may not get a check from the government for several weeks yet how ever when I get paid at the end of the month I'll probably send some money home if there is any available means. I don't see where I can use any money out here except maybe a few dollars a month for candy & other little items. Our meals cost 75 cents a day.

LATER: Sameday.
Still waiting around for my boys to come in. Meanwhile I'm getting a little of this sun every once in a while. My face is plenty red but not burned. My shoulders are getting a little sore tho.

I don't think Jack is on the same island  as I. Does his folks know where he is yet? I sent you a letter with a dollar bill in it marked "Hawaii". Have you gotten it yet? I just wondered if the censor took it out or held up the letter for a while or not.

I wrote to Wither & Jack already. Lee write Waits & Carl soon. Looks like I'm going to be plenty busy around here soon. I may not be able to write as often as I have been. I'll write every chance tho so don't worry.

You notice I changed my address back to the 93rd Sgdn because I'm pretty sure I'll get back with them. Right now I'm assigned to the 28th and I live with the 93rd.(HA) I just go back & forth from one Sgdn to the other. Do just about as I please. Soon as my crew gets here tho I'll be busy flying or going to school!

We had a man from Siapan give us a lecture this morning.

Think he put on my knife  and go out scouting around.   I see some new faces around. Think I better go see who's here.

Tell all hello and everythings OK over here.

Love
Austin

Feby 22, 1945

Well today is Washington's Birthday   Yesterday was Otho's. By the way how is Otho? Is he still down with his dad & Jr. I guess I'll have to write him a letter also one of these days. Its raining this morning and this paper is so soft & damp. I have a job this morning censoring the enlisted mens mail. Maybe I'll get a few pointers on how to write letters now. Cook will surely be here today. Our bombardier got sick in the States at POE and they had to leave him there in the hospital. That is the reason they were so late.

Our POE (Port of Embarkation) was Mather Field Calif. Right close to Sacramento. Right close to Sacramento. It's almost noon now & I've been busy reading letters all morning. Boy you sure can learn just how everyone is getting along & how everyones morale is by censoring the mail. Not many of them try to write anything that they shouldn't. It's funny to see how some try to tell where they are in a round about way.

The $25 I put into the Officers Club back in the States is coming in handy now. We have 6 washing machines about like yours & I'm getting my clothes washed this morning in one of them. We also bought a nice Radio & Record player with that money & it plays over a speaker all day & till about 10 o'clock at nite.

The special services part of army furnish a movie projector & we have a free movie every nite. Right out in the open. A screen tacked up on a tree & everyone sits on the ground or brings a box or chair of some kind with them.(HA) Last nite we saw "My Favorite Wife" a good picture too & the nite before "Going My Way". That made the 3rd time I'd seen that one.

Everyone goes to the movies tho whether they've seen them or not. We have no lights in our tents anyway except flashlights so we might as well see them.

I guess I better close for now & read a few more letters. Then go check the mail to see if I've received any mail myself from the States.

Am standing this sun O.K. & the cool breezes blow most all the time. Its a swell place. So long as it isn't raining.
Solong
Buddie

Feby.  23, 1945
In the Marianas
10 A.M.

Dear Mother & All,

Will begin this letter now although I may not get it finished today. Everything is swell here except Cook hasn't gotten here yet. He started on his last lap yesterday but understand he had an engine failure & I had to return to his take-off field. He may be several days getting here now if he has to have the engine worked on. While I'm waiting for him I'll have a job censoring the mail & doing lots of odd jobs around here. OH! A big formation of 29's just came over the field. They sure look pretty. We are missing a lot of flying & bombing by him being so long coming.

Yesterday I got a wad of mail. But none from you all yet. I understand that, so don't think I'm getting restless about my mail.(HA) I heard from Geraldine C. & she said John B. was a prisoner of Germany. Was sure glad to hear that.

I got my washing done yesterday and it's on the line this morning. I guess sister has been guessing my whereabouts, has she not? There is nothing in this letter to point to my whereabouts so tell her not to try to read between the lines.(HA)

Here's the way I have figured it out. It's all quiet confusing but when its:
6AM where you are its 4PM same day here.
12 Noon " " " " 10 PM " " ".
6 PM where you are its 2 AM next day here.

On our way across we crossed the
International Date line. We took off on Wednesday morning & landed Thursday afternoon
according to the calendar & time.(HA) Altho' we were in the air only 8 hours.

Guess I'll have to stop now for a while. I'll be back tho' so don't go away.

LATER: 12:30 (noon)

Well I got a letter from you and sister just now. Sure am glad to see that we've made connection. Yours was written Feb 15th so I still have lots of older ones yet to come.

I'm not supposed to write on the back of this paper I don't think. The censors don't have time to look around on the back for writing. This stationery is so thick & heavy over 2 pages makes an Air Mail letter pretty heavy.

Tell Miss Josie Belle to write Jack that I'll be seeing him soon, I hope. For him to watch for any B-29s landing there.

I hope she has heard from Jack lately. I'm sitting out in the shade under a tree now. Brought a cot & my table outside where I could get the cool breeze.

Guess I'll sign off pretty soon. I better write several letters today while I have the time.

Hope John is over that sore throat. That was too much dock hunting NO DOUBT! Am glad Willard got to come home.

Our base is improving every day. We should have a mess hall in a few days. And better living quarters also.

There were three Gobs(?) here today. They had beards a year old it looked like. Don't know where they came from.

Love to all,
Austin

Sunday

Dear Mother & Sis,
Well, I got your second letter yesterday. Guess they will begin coming kinda regular now. This took only 8 days. Also Cook came in yesterday so I'm back with him and in a new tent.

The generals on this island didn't like the idea of us not wearing a complete uniform so I guess I won't be wearing the swim suit so much now. We have to wear khaki shirts & trousers at all times except when taking physical training or manual labor.

Most of the fellows are down looking the plane over now. Some already have their ship names & pictures printed on their planes. We haven't yet.

LATER:
Well it's Monday nite now. The 26th Have been on a little mission since I began the letter. Went to the show tonight & am censoring the mail now. Better say so long & get back to work.

Love to all
Austin

Feb 28, 1945
Marianas

Dearest Mother,

I didn't get time to write to you yesterday, my birthday. We were busy all day. Can't say as I feel any older today but just the same I'm 24 now & that is kinda OLD eh! I feel that I should try to express my gratitude to you for being such a wonderful mother. Thanks a million times for being so patient and kind to us while we were small & didn't understand things too clearly. And for raising us in a Christian home. You're just too sweet for words and I love you.

Things around here are still on the improving side. And one day I got 15 letters. About half them from you & sister. They were sure lots of pleasure to read.

Was glad to get caught up on all this back news that I'd missed. Sure was proud to hear about John B. I also got some of the pictures from Rosie that I took down in Puerto Rico. I guess she will send you the negatives soon. I think she was having done reprints made from some of them.

I used Cooks Type Writer to address the envelope. We went on a mission the other day. Our planes were in on the Big raid on Tokyo the (redacted). None of our planes were damaged & all returned safely. Some were flying for 18 hours tho' I were really tired out when they returned. We weren't in on that one tho'. Cook didn't get here soon enough. The Navy also hit Tokyo the same day. That helped out a lot as the Jap planes couldn't get up to our planes on account of such bad weather.

Tell sister she only use the second when writing to her - How is John Winn's cold getting along? Hope he is over with it by now. How's Grandpa also? Am glad you found something you could use in the foot-lockers.

Oh my radio Operator & I went out & climbed a Coconut tree got 10 nice Coconuts. We ate & drank the milk from one. It sure is good. We'll be careful not to get sick on them tho so don't worry.(HA).

When you get the negatives, have about 3 extra pictures of me with the stick in my hand down by the beach made & send them to me. I have had several request for pictures lately.(HA)

This is about all the time I've got now. Hope they've heard from Jack by now. I write to these guys out here I put "Inter Island" on my envelope & it's suppose to go to them without first returning to San Francisco then back. As yet tho I haven't had an answer. Wither has 11 missions in now & has moved to the Philippines.

Tell all hello.
Love,
Austin
