October 1945

194510
October 2, 1945 Hello Folks, Still in San Francisco. Having a pretty fair time. Getting used to the States again. Have my clothes cleaned & pressed before I get home if they aren't already 'clean.' How's Johnie Cake? Well we stop for now. Just a hello note. How's your throat, Mother? Love to all, Austin
________________
OCT 4, 1945 Hello, I'm leaving S.F. tonite to go to Camp Chaffey, Ark. By TRAIN. I Report there Oct 8th - See you soon! Love Austin
________________
LT. Austin H. Winn O-774857 100 TROOPS IN CHANG'T DOUNGE APO-337 907M S.F. TUCSON, ARIZONA Hello Folks, Traveling thru Tuscon Ariz. Its 12:30 & I'm Eating Chow now' Best Wishes to all Love Austin
