
Army Air Field
Great Bend
Jan 10 1945

Hello Mother & all,
How's everything around home?
Got back here O.K., and as I predicted everyone else was gone - 
We're around a few days tho.
We didn't have a very good connection on the phone did we? Capt Cook and most of the other fellows got back at 2 o'clock yesterday but they aren't here yet. They are probably here now - They had some bad weather in the East, we gotta go out to the flight line now & see what's going on - Capt. Cook is in charge.
All the fellows in our Sqn. now.
While we're here - How's John Wynn?
Bye now
Love,
Austin

Great Bend Army Air Field Jan 11, 1945

Dear Mother & Sir,

Well what's new? Good bit of cold weather around here - What's John Winn doing that isn't funny? We are all busy around here. I think see go where I told you for a little more flying. I got your letters yesterday.

Am sending some papers home that you can keep so you will be well informed.

I'll be writing to you again soon. I also sent the foot locker to you today. You can look through it & take some clothes out to hang up - Well we gotta Kello 918 hours. Tell all hello to Lode,

Austin

--- PAGE 4 ---
Dear Mother And Sis,

Well what's going on around home now? Might be a few days before you're hearing from me again - Everything is just fine here though and I'm enjoying the warm sun - Some of the fellows are going swimming this afternoon.

How's John Wilma and grandpa getting along? Hope grandpa is able to keep up now. I mailed you the key to my locker from the base before leaving.

Because of censorship regulations, I cannot write much about myself or what I'm doing at present so will say bye-bye - Love & all and tell the neighbors hello.
Bye 'Austin

--- PAGE 5 ---
Jan. 15, 1945

Dear Sister Spiller,

How is everyone today? Pretty warm here but it's cold that night. We're having a good time at 3:30 in the morning. So far, Tony says an A-OK. I guess you'll have to wait and see what you get from mother.

Keep your fingers crossed for me. How are things going over there? Hope you got all the letters. See you later.

Love,
Jane Lee

--- PAGE 6 ---
Well Gals,

We're in Ohio again. The weather is so bad back at Waycross, Ga. That we can't get to there. The snow is still deep and it's Friday a vacation also.

This afternoon we are going to Miami Beach visiting. We didn't have the proper clothes to wear again. It's too hot here for this time of year. We have 20 mm with us so it must rainier to stay in this warm climate.

We've been drinking quite a lot and kids - pineapple juice by the pail. We had bananas & coconuts just out the door.

--- PAGE 7 ---
You could stand on the barracks porch to pick bananas from the stalk - I have a coconut in planning on sending home - & have several rolls of film to have developed also will send them to you as soon as possible.

Well my dinner is here so I better stop this to eat all 3 us are sitting at the table writing while waiting for our order. Well all hello love Austin
