John D. Wynne

Military man in Austin H. Winn’s letters 2

Mentioned in letters as: John D. Wynne

In the letters

Colonel Wynne, director of training; Austin told his family the class book would show the officers’ pictures.

Researched note

  • Name: John D. Wynne
  • Branch of Service: U.S. Army Air Forces / Air Corps
  • Activity During WWII: Director of Training (signed training memo as Lt. Col., Air Corps); Austin later called him Colonel Wynne at the Texas advanced field and said the class book would show officers’ pictures.

The letters collection includes an official training memo block: “JOHN D. WYNNE, Lt. Col., Air Corps, Director of Training.” A later Army, Navy, Air Force Journal note (1960) reports Col. John D. Wynne assigned as Director of Training, Operations Division, Twelfth Air Force—consistent with a career training officer of the same name. Full wartime station bio beyond the memo/letters not independently reconstructed here.

Registry search attempted; no ABMC plaque located.
