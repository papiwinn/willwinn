Mentioned in letters as: Alexander Alexion

In the letters

Named once among the officers on Austin’s new B-29 crew in July 1945 (with K. G. Smith, Doganges, and Butler).

Researched note

Named on Austin’s July 1945 new B-29 officer crew list (with K. G. Smith, Doganges, Butler) while Austin was flying as an airplane commander in the 19th Bomb Group. No independent public biography matched. Austin’s own service with the group is corroborated by the City of Memphis plaque listing Austin H. Winn.
