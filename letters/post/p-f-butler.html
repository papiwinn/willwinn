P. F. Butler

Military man in Austin H. Winn’s letters 1

Mentioned in letters as: P. F. Butler

In the letters

Named once among the officers on Austin’s new July 1945 crew. Matches “Paul Butler” on the City of Annapolis crew photograph in manuscript chapter 18.

Researched note

FACT from the letters: On 4 July 1945 Austin listed “P.F. Butler” among the four officers on his new crew.

FACT from the photograph: The ink under the kneeling man at the far left appears to read “Paul Butler.”

HYPOTHESIS: That “Paul Butler” is the letters’ P. F. Butler. The photograph supplies Paul; the letters supply only P. F.

FACT from the roster: The 20th Air Force association’s 19th Bomb Group B index does not list Butler.

HYPOTHESIS, not claimed: A 2006 obituary of Paul F. Butler of Bridgewater, Massachusetts, says he was born in Cambridge on 1 July 1922, Harvard College class of 1944, and “served in the U.S. Air Force as a radar navigator bombarder, rising to the rank of Lieutenant Colonel.” It does not name the 19th Bomb Group, the 93rd Bomb Squadron, a B-29, Guam, Annapolis, or a crew. Harvard plus radar only suggests the unnamed special instrument operator in the 29 July 1945 letter, and Cambridge is not Annapolis, so he is not the bombardier.

Sources: 20af.org — 19th Bomb Group roster (B); Current Obituary — Paul F. Butler, 2006.
