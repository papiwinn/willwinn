Warren D. Withee

Military man in Austin H. Winn’s letters 30

Mentioned in letters as: Warren D. Withee

In the letters

Oshkosh roommate from early cadet days through Roswell (Withee / Wither / Withie). Austin saw him off by train, later heard he became a co-pilot for a hometown friend, and still wrote him from Guam.

Researched note

Letters firmly place Warren D. Withee as Austin’s Oshkosh, Wisconsin roommate from cadet days through Roswell; later a co-pilot for a hometown friend. Public Withee pilots found online (George Withee B-29; Bob Withee fighter) are different men. No ABMC plaque or Oshkosh newspaper bio matched.

Not independently verified beyond the letters. Registry search attempted.
