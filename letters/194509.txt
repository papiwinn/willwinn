1945 Sept 3rd

Dearest Folks,

Received R Nice letters from you today - You seemed to expect me to just start right off home as soon as the War was over. Well there still hasn't been any definite news about that.

Yesterday V.J day & Sunday & was back over Japan. We were up there about 500 strong in a display Air Power. We buzzed out Tokyo & vicinity for 3 hours while The Emperor & Mrs Arthur were signing the Peace terms

We got a good look at the cities we had been bombing - They were really destroyed. Pictures will be in Life Magazine Soon - Try to get one

--- PAGE 2 ---
I suppose that will be my last mission to The Empire.
Here's another picture by our crew. This time we got the Co-Pilot Smith into it. He is the first from the left standing—
We have to fill out another questionnaire by the 25 Sept. stating if we want to stay in the Army during the present emergency—I was unsure "No"—I will try to stay get out as soon as possible.
Major Cook took my plane and went to California on a business trip for the Army. The whole crew was made up of Californians—They are staying at Diego. You you're able to read this scribbling? I'm in a big hurry so I have excuse the writing.

--- PAGE 3 ---
What's little Johnnie doing now? I have a pair of ear Rugs & bought today & also another nutlack & broceler but they aren't as nice as the ones I sent. Must be going to see grandpa hello. Hope he feels well.
Love always,
Austin

--- PAGE 4 ---
Dear Mother and Sis,
Sept 5, 1945.

Raining again today - This makes about 7 days in a row without any sunlight. Everyone is getting restless & wanting to come home. Capt Handwerker is leaving today for home. He is getting out of the Army also I hear! He has enough points to be released.

We intended to have a big parade & review today but the rain made them postpone it until tomorrow.
Have you & sister been to any shows at all lately? Why don't you get our a little one in a while?

What is Hugkey Chester doing now? Is he working in Memphis or going to school!

--- PAGE 5 ---
I suppose Jack is still having a good time isn't he!
I wrapped up another necklace & bracelet to mail home. You may get it in a few days! Keep it on and get some little catches to hook it on with.
Well she say bye-bye ya now.
Love to all,
Austin

--- PAGE 6 ---
Sept 8, 1945
Sham.

Dear Mother and Sister,

Today I was lucky to get two letters from you. Also got a couple others. One from Jack.

Things are about the same around here. Except it didn't rain much today only a shower late in the afternoon. We had a big Base Ball game here today! The players are all Major League Ball players that have been drafted into the Army. That's all they do now is play ball, I guess. They are all the way from Pitts to one major. There will be another game tomorrow. I never did see such a crowd as attended the game today. The whole field was surrounded with spectators - All the way around.

--- PAGE 7 ---
We had a little bad news today—They had intended sending all crews with 31 missions or over home in a few days but if all fell thru to day due to be tie-up in transportable facilities on Biplan The fellows that left here 2 to 4 weeks ago to go home are still in Biplan waiting to be shipped out. So much it will the best anyway Now Dave can wait here until the bottle neck is cleared to may get a straight trip from Drew instead of having to go via Biplan. Hope so anyway.

We haven't received those pictures I was having developed slow process isn't it However I am getting 9 rolls of film developed tomorrow nite by a fellow & show here

--- PAGE 8 ---
Course they will only be negative but I can have prints made as soon as I get home—

Jack says he wants to know if he gets our mail in time to start West and crop then glad they bought the farmman dish. How are the Simpson's getting along? Oh! You asked about Lucas. He is fine & dandy back from the hospital and back on duty. My uncle is okay too.

Say, you haven't gotten any letters lately from Kathy or any of the girls have you? Just wondered.

Well it's time to say goodbye.
Hope all are well - Tell Mr. Keaton hello for me -
Love to All,
Austin

--- PAGE 10 ---
Dear Mother & Sis,

Today is another rainy day. This morning all the Pilots in the Wing got together for a talk by Colonel Storin on Flying Safety etc. It seems our accident rate is higher now than before the war ended due to carelessness on the part of some individuals. Today Sunday, they are having a Funeral & Burial of one crew that was killed a couple days ago in a landing accident. And there have been 2 P-51s crashed this week also.

Yesterday being a nice sunny day I went to the beach in the afternoon. Had a nice time & took up my last roll of film. While we were in swimming, the M.P.s came around blowing their whistles & made everyone get out as 2 sharks had gotten inside the reef where we were swimming.
(Over)

--- PAGE 11 ---
At the meeting this morning I saw 5 of my old classmates from Santa Ana & the other flying schools we've been through. They are flying off the same field but it's so big you can't get around to see many people other than your own group. We have four groups in the 314th Wing - 19th, 29th, 39th, 330th groups and each group has 4 squadrons in it.

I got the Lauderdale County Jungs this morning. They don't come very regularly. I saw where Harold Evans & James Fitzhugh both Ripley pilots are stationed at Montgomery Alabama. That's a B-29 Transition School.

The meeting so much carried on big zinnias & Jac over so we pulled one bed up & planted New Glouster in the bed. My Zinnia had lots of summer varieties - Blue Bonnets, etc.

The fellows in the hut are practicing on their saxophone, drums, and trumpet. So I'm sitting on the porch.

--- PAGE 12 ---
It's time for the showers to come on now so see be stopping - We aren't doing anything much but eating & sleeping - Some are flying formation almost every day here as I have 38 missions & haven't had to fly any yet yet. We have lots of crews over here who didn't get any missions in & some who got only a few 1-10 etc. They are going to have to remain overseas for support for a good while.

How are all the crops and livestock this year? Haven't got a radio in our hut now so seldom hear the news now a days. Tell all hello.

Best wishes
Jane,
Austin

Sept. 10, 1945
Quam—

Dear Mother & sis,

How's everyone today? Things are about the same here.

Still raining every day & muddy as the Mississippi bottom gumbo. Did you receive the little boy with the necklace & bracelet in it? I mailed it Air Mail a few days ago.

I got a lot of negatives back today and am trying to get a few prints made of them. I have our crew picture that was taken at Great Bend. Guess you recognize Bondner, Cook, Armstrong, & Phillips—!! All standing—

Did you get a crew picture like the one I gave Olette? If not, I still have the negative so we can have more made.

--- PAGE 15 ---
Dear Mother and Sis,

Rain, rain, rain - Still raining every day and getting muddier all the time. Am sure glad this war is over so we'd see flying in all this rain. That makes landings & takeoffs dangerous.

I have been put on a list of persons to be sent home soon. So many before long!! I have some more films (negatives) in sending along. You can send them off to be developed if you like. Just be sure to keep the negatives from getting lost.

How is everything around here? There are only 2 from my crew going home soon - Feigle & myself. He had 32 missions & I got 33. Guess John enjoys being able to tell the gas station "Fill 'er up" again.

--- PAGE 16 ---
Has it started getting cool there yet? I had to sleep under a blanket here last nite.

Has any cotton started opening yet? What kind of crop does John expect this year?

Is the Air Base at Halbe still in operation? I heard something about them moving it seems.

Well, this looks like all for now. Love to all,
Austin

--- PAGE 19 ---
Dear Mother and Sis,

Only time for a few lines before bedtime tonite. Happened to get hold of a picture of our little garden beside the hut. You can see the tomatoes etc in the foreground. We had another patch besides this.

We haven't done much today. I went down the island to Waycross, Ga., to buy some khaki clothing. Also got about 3 more rolls of the film I was having developed from the developer. I'll probably keep these & bring them with me. I sent you 5 rolls I developed here a few days ago. Let me know when you get them.

I am still hoping for a chance to leave here. Just seems like a slow process, but don't worry it's getting out of there some way soon.

--- PAGE 24 ---
Dear Folks,

Well at last something has started in regards to coming home - I have left my old habitation & moved into a large warehouse awaiting transportation to the States - So if you don't hear from me for as much as a week you can figure I'm aboard the boat for home. Yipee!

There's a chance I may return by air also - God knows how long we'll be waiting here but we already left the 149th Group & finished my processing.

Was really surprised to hear about Jack & Evelyn. Had he already gone to Pennsylvania? Sure must have had a quick change of mind - I thought Athinae Marie was his last girl!

--- PAGE 25 ---
Well it still raining almost continually around here. You remember the little gold knife you gave me for Christmas in 1943! I lost it in my moving around here yesterday. Sure did hate to lose it. But always carried it in my pocket as a little fingernail knife (he).

We have plenty of books to read here at the lights are much cutta than in our old hut.

Well we stop for this time.
With love to all,
Austin

P.S. Yo need to write to me any more over here.

--- PAGE 26 ---
Dear Mother & Sis,

Just reporting that I'm OK and still here on Guam. I have a good chance of getting a ride out there. Meal Room by plane tho so here's hoping.

I received your letter that said you were going to have your tonsils removed. Hope everything's okay now & you're feeling well.

Sure is warm here tonight. Ain't making a detective story to pass the time away. We have a car in a large warehouse where we are living until we get transportation home.

Think there's any news to write. Hope everyone is feeling fine around home. Now I'm glad y'all got the car when you did.

Solary & City Nite, Love,
Austin

GUAM THURSDAY SEPT. 27, 1945

DEAR FOLKS,

CAN YOU IMAGINE THEM STILL KEEPING ME HERE ON GUAM ALL THIS LONG?

I HAVE BEEN PUT ON ATC ORDERS THO SO THAT MEANS THAT I AM ALMOST SURE TO LEAVE HERE BY PLANE. THE SOONER THE BETTER... HOW IS EVERY ONE AT HOME GETTING ALONG?

I RECEIVED MOTHERS LETTER TODAY AND AM GLAD TO HEAR THE OPERATION WAS A SUCCESS. DID GRANDPA STAY IN TIPTON LONG ENOUGH TO LET YOUR THROAT GET WELL? THE WEATHER IS REALLY SULTRY HERE TODAY. ITS A HARD MATTER TO KEEP ENOUGH CLEAN CLOTHES TO WEAR WHILE WE'RE WAITING FOR A RIDE.

WHAT IS BILLY S. DOING THESE DAYS? DID YOU SAY THAT HE IS A CIVILIAN AGAIN? AND HOW IS JACK AND EVELYN? THAT REALLY WAS A SURPRISE TO EVERYONE I CAN IMAGINE. ISN'T JACK GOING TO GET A DISCHARGE?

YOU SAID YOU HADN'T HEARD WHERE HANDWERKER HAD GOTTEN HOME YET.

WELL HE ISN'T. WE HEARD TODAY THAT HE IS STILL ON SAIPAN WAITING FOR A BOAT. SO MABY I'LL GET HOME FIRST AFTER ALL. I'D MUCH RATHER WAIT FOR A PLANE HERE ON GUAM THAN FOR A BOAT ON SAIPAN. THE TRIP BY BOAT WOULD BE A NEW EXPERIENCE I SUPPOSE BUT I DON'T THINK I WOULD LIKE IN THIS CASE.

WE HAVE PLENTY OF RECREATION FACILITIES HERE IN OUR WAREHOUSE. OH! LAST NITE A FEW OF US GOT TOGETHER AND FROZE TWO GALLONS OF ICE CREAM. WE USED ICE CREAM POWDER AND WATER WITH ONE PINT OF CONDENSED MILK. IT WAS THE BEST CREAM I HAD EATER SINCE I'VE BEEN OVER HERE. I LICKED THE PADDLE AND IT REMINDED ME OF THE ICE CREAM SUPPERS THAT WE USED TO HAVE DOWN AT THE MACK CHURCH. LOOKS LIKE THIS IS IT FOR TONITE. SO I'LL BE SEEING YOU SOON. I'LL CALL YOU WHEN I GET TO SAN FRANCISCO.

LOVE TO KELLY, ALL,

AUSTIN

Dear Folks,

I'm back in the good ole U.S.A. Came by plane from Guam. Sent you a telegram this morning instead of telephoning as it take so long to make connections by phone.

Well here's the dope - I reported to the Port Absorption at Hamilton Field, Calif. They will have our orders ready Oct. 4th sending me to some Redistribution Center in Western Ark. I don't remember the name even.
