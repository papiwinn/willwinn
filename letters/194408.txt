Dear Folks,

How is everyone today? Everything fine around here - I flew last nite til 12:30. Had 3 teeth pulled this morning and am flying in the morning so I'll have to get up at 4:30 A.M. Saturday Aug 5

Hello Folks,

How is everyone today? I'm O.K., except that & just took two more photos a few minutes ago so expect my arms to be sore by tonite.

I'm flying this afternoon & then have all day Sunday till 10 o'clock Monday off - if my arms aren't too sore I may go to El Paso or Roswell one of the other. I still have to go back in another week & get a typhus shot & then after that get a yellow fever shot. So I'll be all plugged full 9 holes before I leave here.

I have some things you probably have to have sent

To me before the end of this month - We are supposed to have full equipment in dress in any part of the world with us at all times. So you can get my short coat and wool gloves out to send me. And let you know if he's sending anything home then y'all can use the same thing to send them back in.

The short coat is the cream colored coat about finger tip length and the gloves are in the till D and the trunk in my room - I don't need the scarf - I have so much stuff now & can't pack it over with me. We got to get another heavy pair of shoes & leggings, raincoat, wool underwear etc.

Monday — Aug 7, 1944

Hello Mother & all,

How is everything today? We had a nice rain last nite & I got caught out in it - Didn't get much wet. Sure was a good nite for sleeping. I go to ground school this afternoon & fly tonite. Yesterday I wrote 8 letters so I'm almost caught up but not quite.

I guess John is getting some good wood, and you've already ordered your coal. That's good.

The War News in Europe looks good - We have a big map with the allied line marked on it and it keeps them busy trying to keep up with the armies just changing lines on the maps. I saw some good pictures of the invasion at the show last nite, pictures of the invasion on June 6th & also some war news pictures of the Democratic Convention.

I took 3 shirts & 2 trousers to the cleaners a week ago Friday & went after them this morning. If they aren't back yet, I'll put 3 trousers & 1 shirt in today so I'm almost clothesless now (HAHA). The laundry has never come either since I've been here.

1944
Tuesday August 15

Dear Folks,

Everyone is talking about the invasion of Southern France this morning. The intelligence officers are having to put up another map now to show this part of France.

I went down to the flight line at 3:30 this morning and they didn't have enough planes ready to fly to go around this morning so I got back about 6 AM. Went back to bed till around 9.

We have our final exam in engineering this afternoon. The rumors is still going. Sure arend about another delay in route. Sure hope it's true.

I've just eaten dinner & it's almost time to get to school. So see be going now.

Love,
Austin
