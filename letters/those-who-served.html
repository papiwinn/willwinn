St. John Handwerker (John J. “Jim” Handwerker) 11

Mentioned in letters as: St. John Handwerker

In the letters

Fellow B-29 airplane commander from Memphis; Austin wrote about the City of Memphis on their field, asked his mother about newspaper clippings, and arranged for Handwerker to carry film/pictures home.

Researched note

Public plaque and nose-art records identify B-29A 42-93917 “City of Memphis” (also “Nipp On Ese Nipper”), 19th Bomb Group, 93rd Squadron. A Memphis plaque dedicated by the B-29 Club of Memphis (1998) lists John J. “Jim” Handwerker as pilot and Austin H. Winn among the other pilots/crews associated with the ship. Sources: readtheplaque.com; usaaf-noseart.co.uk.

A. V. Winn (Alden Vernon Winn) 28

Mentioned in letters as: A. V. Winn

In the letters

Fellow cadet/airman Austin first met as A.V. Winn from Washington state (also Alden / Alvin in the letters). They trained in the same world, kept writing, and later met on Guam—A.V. in B-24s with missions in, then on Okinawa. Austin asked how John and A.V. looked after returning to the States.

Researched note

WWII Memorial Registry honoree plaque (model entry for this page):
  • Name: Alden Vernon Winn
  • Branch of Service: U.S. Army Air Force
  • Hometown: Wallula, Washington
  • Honored By: Brad Winn (Grandson)
  • Activity During WWII: Co-pilot, B-24 aircraft, 494th Bomb Group (H), 7th Air Force, 1945. Group stationed in Anguar Island, Palau Islands, and Okinawa. Missions include Philippine Islands, Japan, China, and certain Pacific islands.
ABMC WWII Registry plaque (honoree_id=2199814)

Letters say Walla Walla; the plaque lists Wallula, Washington (nearby). No other public bio page was required beyond this registry plaque.
