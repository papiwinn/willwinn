October 2, 1945

Hello Folks,

Still in San Francisco.

Having a pretty fair time.
Getting used to the States again. Have my clothes cleaned & pressed before I get home if they aren't already 'clean.'

How's Johnie Cake?

Well we stop for now. Just a hello note.
How's your throat, Mother?
Love to all,
Austin
