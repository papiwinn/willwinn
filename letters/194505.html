Tuesday May 1, 1945

Tuesday May 1, 1945 Dear Mother And Sis, A pretty day here today. Glad to get your letters yesterday. And also I got two copies of the Lauderdale County Enterprise. I've been busy reading them this morning. Went to a medical lecture earlier today. Expect to fly this afternoon. Test HP. It's 9 AM now. We usually get our mail around 10:30 AM and 4:30 PM. Lots of packages the last few days but still no sheets as yet. What's the latest from Jack? Maby you saw the write-up in the paper a few days ago about the B-29, The City of Memphis!! It's here on this field and Lt. Handwerker is the airplane Commander. He is from Memphis. The paper kinda mis-represented the facts and all it talked about was some Colonel from Memphis who had nothing to do with that plane -- or with naming it. I wrote Otho a letter last night. first time I've written him in a long time. Is he still working on the boat? Later Tuesday nite: Well I've been up flying this afternoon - Had a nice time - We took about 4 C-Bs up for a ride. On the first take-off we had a plug come out of one engine & lost about 40 gallons of oil so had to land again & stop up the hole & refill with oil. Then we took off again and broke in the new engine I wrote about earlier - Glad to see the City of Memphis picture you sent, I wrote something about them earlier in this letter but it was another article I spoke of. Received the last pictures of John Winn Friday they are sure good. I'll send some back to you. Also got the watermelon seed from both you & Mary F. thanks The garden looks swell We already have watermelons with leaves on them too. Radish about 1 in". The first flowers I planted are sure slow almost coming up. We have at least 10 flower packets of seed now. The other fellows have been getting seed also. Better go now, Love Austin Dearest Folks Thursday 9 missions 10:30 PM Marianas Dear Folks, May 5th 11 A.M. Saturday 10 Missions Hello again! I started to write this letter Thursday nite before I left on the last mission but didn't get time to write - I got to bed about 2 AM this morning & slept till 10AM. Feel OK now & pretty rested up. The showers had all the water turned off so I went back to the helmet again to get cleaned up. I got the Watermelon & Muskmelon seed you sent, Thanks. We bombed the Japanese airfields again - We were attacked by a few fighters but they weren't too sharp. They did kinda give us a surprise on their first attack. The news sounded grand - we listened to the news on the way to Japan and heard of Hitler's and Goebels' death & also of the surrender of the German army in Italy & Southern Austria. Hope the peace conference is doing a good job. Oh, guess who I saw Thursday! I was lying in my sack dozing off before the mission & A.V. Winn Comes in it woke me up. Sure was glad to see him - he's the kid from Walla Walla Washington. He is in B-24 & has 7 missions in. That makes all 3 of the Winns on this island now. (Ha) He's only temporarily here tho.I took him down & showed him our B-29 - He was really impressed over such a big plane. Well it's time for the mail now so I'll rush this on over there and see if I've gotten any mail from you today. Love to all; Austin What is John doing these days? Guess what! Tucker the Starbuck chewed something up. And last week read a book. Now we just keep a note to tell that we have to fly a mission. Maximum 50 missions. There's a possibility of getting a rest leave after 15. Way to go, Jane.

May 5, 1945

May 5, 1945 Dear Mother and Sis, Wrote you a letter yesterday with the same date on it but was mistaken - Got mixed up on what day it was? Should have been Friday, the 4th instead. It's raining here this morning. I just wrote Wilbur a letter. How is everyone around Cherry getting along? In the last seed you sent, I got some watermellon, muskmellon and I suppose the little round seed are turnip seed. Is that right? What is John doing these days? Guess both tractors are kept pretty busy getting the corn land ready for planting. It's time for mail now so I'll just send a note -- We are told that we will have to fly a maximum of 35 missions. There is a possibility of getting a rest after 15 tho. Well so long, Love to All, Austin

Sunday, May 6th

Sunday, May 6th Marianas How's every one today. We went to another airfield this morning & flew a 29 back to the home base. We had an awful bad rain last nite & as the planes were coming back from the mission, only one of our planes landed at this field. They are scattered throughout the Marianas and one other field on this island. We didn't go on this mission. The boys are having a volleyball game this afternoon. Guess I'll have to go out and play with them. I got my first letter from Jack today. glad to hear he's back in the states. He has to have another operation. Says he feels fine tho. Well I must go now. Tell all hello. Things will be hopping around here again soon. The missions will get rougher if we don't go to the Kyucher(?). We are glad to get those kind of missions The strategic targets are better protected by the Japs. Love to all, Austin

May 9th, 1945

May 9th, 1945 Marianas 10 missions Dearest Mother of All, Today is V-E day - Was sure glad to hear the news on the radio - Mr. Truman is supposed to talk tonight at 11PM Don't know if we will be awake to hear him or not. Today we bombed a Jap island for practice. Had a very nice trip & got back about 3PM. We had chicken which was very good. Received a letter from Jack yesterday. Did you get the pictures I sent you? Think I sent 2. Boy, Hughey really has some fancy articles in the Hi-Life, doesn't he! The flowers around home must be blooming now. That is lots of them - I could see several in the picture you sent to John Winn. We have our name painted on the plane now. Also a bomb for each mission & 5 Jap flags for 3 fighter planes our tail gunner shot down. Also each of our own names under our positions The name of our plane is "City of Charlottesville" (Virginia) Well I better get off to sleep now - Good night to all Love and kisses, Austin

Wednesday May 9, 1945 Marianas

Wednesday May 9, 1945 Marianas Hello Mother & Sister, Have been up bombing the islands again for practice. Wrote you a letter last nite but thought I'd drop you another little line this afternoon. Might not be where I can write tomorrow. Heard the news this morning. Wasn't awake last nite to hear The President speak. The mail hasn't come today as yet. Wasn't any morning mail for anyone. Still haven't received any of the packages. I have one coming from you, Robin, Addie, and Dolly too I think. How is the farming coming along? I've been trying to burn off the little warts on my hands with nitric acid. Think I've gotten rid of a couple of them so far. Has Miss Josie Belle come back from Florida yet? Hope Jack's operation is O.K. Tell Grand pa hello & not to try to get around too much when he gets up. Love to all Austin Friday

May 11 1945

May 11 1945 Marianas 11 missions Dear folks, How is everyone today? Got back from the mission on Japs oil refineries & storages last nite - There were over 400 B-29 up there. Boy, I've never seen so many planes in the air together. I landed out Iwo Jima on the return trip. I haven't gotten my mail for yesterday yet. I'll go see if I had any then finish this letter. I believe I just gone up - It's too late to eat breakfast - 8am. I ate a snack last night tho about 10 P.M., later-2 PM. Will I received 2 letters from you today with several clippings included. I got the little memo books too thanks a lot. They are just the right size. You said William Hutch might write to Lowry - Here's his address if you want to give it to him. Col. Leon L. Lowry Commanding Officer 93rd Bomb SQDN APO 246 Unit 2 c/0 PM San Francisco, Calif. --- PAGE 13 --- You've heard me speak of the Airplane Commander from Memphis and his plane? You sent me a picture of this plane—well, he's coming home. He will send you something that I sent home by him. His name is St. John Handwerker. I hear Hilda Gordon is opening up her business again!! He said he might even drive up to our place one day. Guess there will be more news in the paper about him when he gets to Memphis — Try to send me the clippings if you ever them. Well, I'll have to stop about now— Still cloudy & has been rather cool all day so far. We really saw a lot of flak yesterday. Even the Jap ships in the harbor were shooting at us too. The target we were bombing really exploded. The flames & smoke came up over 2000 ft high—the Jap fighters didn't bother us this time. love to all Austin Dear Mother and Sis, Saturday nite And its raining very hard. Got the sheets you sent me today They Sure are nice ones. Tomorrow I have to go down to Wing Headquarters to a meeting. It's 10:30 P.M Now & Most everyone is in bed. I will have to make this Short & Snappy. Saw is John & Johnnie Cake? Has he got both truck running now? Today we didn't get any 1st Class mail at all. Only packages. Tomorrow if I don't have to fly any I may go down to the Beach for a swim. Things are about the same over here I guess So see close for tonite. Love & All Austin Sunday afternoon

May 13

May 13 Mother's Day Dear Mother And Sis, How's everyone today? Got down from a flight around the local area. We went test hopping the plane - took a couple Marines up with us just for the ride. Wrote you a little letter yesterday. Probably won't be able to write tomorrow. Today is Mother's Day - did you get the little present I sent to you? I received a real nice box of cookies & candy too from Addie. It was really wrapped up so nice in cellophane paper. They are good too. One fellow got some new records from home and they are playing them now. Didn't get to go to the theatre today. Haven't seen Mr. Robert Winn any more lately. Well I have to write a couple other letters right quickly, so will say adios for today. Hope you have a lovely day today - know your roses are beautiful too! Love Austin --- PAGE 16 --- Dear Mother & All, How's everything? Gee! I'm sitting here on my bed this morning—Have just had a nice cool shave & shower & well have to write & tell you the news. Sunday Morning May 18

Sunday Morning May 18 Nice morning, however it has been raining a good bit & slept late this morning & didn't get any breakfast. I still have plenty of Cherries & Cookies. In fact I sealed one can of cookies back up & put them away till we eat the others up. Received the packets of flowers sent from you the other day. We have several flowers up but some have to be transplanted. The Phlox & Verbena didn't. We have Celosia, Old Fashioned Gardenias, Zinnias, etc. Don't guess we'll need any more seed for a while. Our tomato patch looks good but there are some worms that are eating the leaves up. Radishes are big enough to eat. Did you get the pictures? Well I'll say Bye now Love to all, Austin Glad to hear Jack is getting along OK. I haven't used the sheets yet (14) those gotten used to sleeping without them. May 21, 1945

May 21, 1945 Marianas Dear Mother & Joe, Didn't get to write to you yesterday. We've been pretty busy lately. We flew 3 times to Japan last week then yesterday we bombed Ick Pop Island for practice. Our engines were getting pretty old. On our last mission we had an oil leak drop out while we were right over Tokyo & lost all the oil out that engine - We feathered it & bombed the city of Hamatshin & flew back to Iwo Jima on 2 engines. Landed there & got the engine fixed & refilled with oil. We flew back home that nite - We are getting 3 new engines on our plane now before we fly it again. You know we put one new one on it a coupla weeks ago, I was awarded the "Air Medal" for the flight over Kobe when we stayed over the target 2 hours. --- PAGE 22 --- I went over this morning to sign up to remain in the Army until Japan is defeated. Not that I would have been able to get out anyway I guess. But we were supposed to put a choice down. I will get an increase in pay this month of about $25. I was the only pilot in my class (44-D) to make 1st Lt. Another guy's promotion came through but he will look after Troyop several weeks ago. Well, he got to go out & play ball with the fellows now. He probably was on the next couple missions while our engines are being changed. Have you heard from Lt. Andrews yet? He is supposed to send you my picture. Tell all hello. Love, Fred --- PAGE 23 --- May 22 1948 What John Wynn doing now? He sure made me out. How is he? Hello Math & Joe! Today I received your letter. Glad to hear from you. Sorry it was late. Have a small one. Then pick up on the wall now. You got a package from Dotty, great they will miss Jane. Jane in the Philippines is so lonely. Well she gotta go way now So bye to ya. Jane & Joe Bell & Dottie today are going up flying again. We're coming right along with the Vengeance changing in excellent condition. (Six) We are flying another plane. Hi Johnny! We are studying in Fort P--- --- PAGE 24 --- Here are those Cheeks Corny Now we had ice cream for a treat around here. They don't have any Cola Sprints except beer. I don't like that Volly Ball. We played none today. You need an Uncle Joe to make some more Jigs. We made some more Jigs that are ground & cold. Tokyo was in the snow again. All the crews became sick from being cold and wet. So we had a row so we had 3 in a row except me, we have been on the ship about two weeks now. This one. I got a little from you nite to sleep so it's good mixed this one. Today thank you for the little ice cream & bread it very much. --- PAGE 25 --- Dear Sir & Mother, Today has been a very pleasant one. If Jack has written lately how is he? Thought I'd just drop a line or two to write before going to bed. Know I won't get to write you again for a couple days maybe three so don't worry and if you don't hear from me go ahead. Today we went down to the beach & went swimming. Went to another Anfield where C.A.V. was but didn't see him anywhere. We ate supper there & we had beef steak which was very good. Also drank a couple cold Coca Colas while there which tasted swell. Received your letter today. They come very regularly sometimes they come by twice tho. Well, I'll say nite about now. What's Big John doing these days? Tell him hello for me. Love to all, Austin --- PAGE 26 ---
