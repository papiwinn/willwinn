1944

OFFICERS' CLUB
Roswell Army Air Field
Roswell, New Mexico
Thursday June 1st

Hello Folks,

How's things around home? We are having our usual afternoon dust storm and this one seems a little dustier than usual - Anyway Today is gas mask day so if it gets too bad we can don these things. Every Thursday we pack our gas masks around with us all day. We had a little gas alert this morning while we were in ground school and everyone had to put the masks on. We looked like a bunch Japs sitting there working on a navigation problem.

I just got my hair cut and washed - Am going to link in a jiffy if am flying tonight. I sent you a couple bonds as I had some excess money when I got my check this month.

Dear Mother and All,

Dear Mother,

Well I couldn't get off to come to the Halls Base today - I slept all morning and am having ground school this afternoon. We have our final test in Navigation - May be lucky the next time - I have 17 hours in now & fly Sunday nite so I'll finish my 105 hours in one more trip after Sunday.

We have a nice time up there sometimes - For instance we have 5 radios - We tune one in on Tokyo, one in San Francisco, and one on the local Radio Station at Carlsbad. Then keep one tuned in on the airfield tower so we can listen to most any thing.

It's a beautiful day here today—
but everything is growing on the farm too—I enjoyed your letters yesterday.

Well anyway it looks like we'll sit

except maybe we'll go up as a bombardier

We were scheduled for bomb training this morning but I was excused as they had more boys than they had trainers—

Hello all,

Just a line so you can hear from me today - haven't done much of anything today - played a few games & wrote some letters. Read a story for a change (HA) a little short one in a Saturday Evening Post.

You read of the plane in the paper you read about last October - I'm sure around. Hope grandpa is OK after all that work & then going on the creek. Besides, we had fish for dinner today - Friday is Fish day in the Army anyway!

--- PAGE 17 ---
Tonight we had peach ice cream & cake - Oh sister I am proud of the pictures you & John Withers. I have your picture up in a frame and I put one of John Withers in my pocket book. He has to be careful who sees it in my pocket book now won't he! Of the girls will think I'm married HA
I'm going to make this short because Withers is leaving tomorrow & we may go into town tonight and see a show or bowl some - I haven't seen him all day yet.
Tomorrow I have PT at 10 o'clock and that's all I do & think. I'm really getting a vacation almost, will soon be busy again though
I'm sure Love to all Austin

Hello Folks,

How is everything around home? Today I have been going to school this morning. We had Aircraft Recognition Code and Bombing lectures. It was a story of a B-17 on a trip over Germany, a real action picture put out by the War Department.

I just got a hair-cut. I want to write Carl & Bill Simpson a letter. I couldn't get any V-Mail at the PX though - Lee got some rest time in town though. I heard from Billy this morning and was surprised. He said Sister had written to him. I'm going over to get the afternoon mail.

We have had Maintenance of Aircraft today - Mechanics explained all about how to fix things about the planes etc. Studied the engines & how to change a tire and fix brakes etc.

I'm sending one of my navigation problems along with this. This is a record which the navigator keeps on a cross-country flight as you go along. This was a flight from Wichita, Kansas to Dyersburg to Montgomery, Ala.

Thursday June 22

Dear Mother & all,
Just a little note this morning so you can at least hear from me. I just ate breakfast to am in the lobby of the mess hall (HA) Have to go to P.T. in a few minutes. We are wearing out gas masks again today - every Thursday.

How is everything around home? Looks like another hot day here. We had bananas this morning with grape nuts - Our old stand-by for breakfast is fried eggs & hot cakes (every day since I've been here) (molasses) honey too! Have a variety at all other meals - The breakfast is OK, too because there is always several kinds of cereals & milk, coffee toast, jelly.

All done this in the P.O. on my way back to the barracks - I'm glad go way back and there to change clothes for P.T.
Love to all
Austin
