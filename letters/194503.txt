Dear Mother & Sister,

Will have time for only a few little lines this morning—Guess you've missed a few days hearing from me. Well I've really been busy & doing a lot of flying.

Today we're going to ground school. Yes, we still have to study up on this flying & bombing—learn the latest stuff from other crews etc. That have been over Japan recently. Guess you read where we destroyed 240 city blocks in Tokyo the other day—Well our planes from this group were in on that raid. Our ship wasn't tho. Cook got here too late as I've said before. I've been on 3 bombing missions so far but not on any over Japan itself. Soon tho I will. We've been up around where Jack is.

I had forgotten what the date is even—Notice I put Feb. as the date at first—it's hard to keep up with the day of the week also.

--- PAGE 2 ---
How is every one around the neighborhood? I will be too busy to write much over 2 times a week it looks like.

We are one of the lead crews in this group. It's a pretty important job. All the navigation & bombing is dependent on the lead ship since all the others follow along in formation.

Things are going O.K. One day we got to go swimming - we were actually practicing ditching drill - getting in out of the rubber life rafts etc. It was lot of fun tho. We climbed some coconut trees and ate some coconuts also. Came a big raid last nite & ruined part of the outdoor movie that was going on at the time - I've gotta stop now. So Bye. Tell all hello.

Love,
Austin
Am getting mail pretty good now

March 12, 1945
Marianas

Hello Sister Hale,
How's everyone? Guess you've been reading a bit of the girls we kindled in Tokyo & Nagoya! We lost some planes and we got through without a scratch.
Today we moved into a quonset hut. The crew that lived there was lost on their first mission - All fellows I know too. One of Coots big buddies from Alamogordo of all along the line. The crew I rode with from the States was also lost.
We had a mighty nice mission & put our bombs exactly on 30° the target area. Our gunners are getting promotions to Sgt.

--- PAGE 6 ---
I got a letter from Wilbur today. Got to try to take a shower somewhere tonight - I sure am tired & sleepy.

Oh, yes you asked me several questions in your last letter. Your Air Mail letters come to me in about 8 days. The V-Mail usually comes without even being photographed - your mail to me is not censored - My mail to you is spot-checked.

Everyone here has to wear their guns around everywhere we go now. I carry a .45 pistol.

This place is a nice place to sleep now after staying in the trees so long. We will have water soon The tower is already built - Bob landed on an island with 2 engines gone the other night. Same as Jacks - Bob is the guy in the little picture with me.

March 15/1945
Marianas

Dear Mother & all,

How is everyone today? Guess you've seen where I've been pretty busy lately - I went to Waycross the other nite. Then to Osaka on our way home from the raid & listened to the news broadcasts of Washington announced that B-29s hit Osaka Wednesday morning - & was still flying & not even half way back to my base. We encountered no trouble on either raid - we did get pretty scared once or twice but thats just natural. The big search light shooting up through the nite looking for your floor. They caught us once I followed us right over the targes. We couldn't dodge

them as that time because we had to keep straight ahead on the bomb run. As soon as we dropped our bombs tho we were weaving around and lost the lights - The Auto Aircraft guns on the ground were throwing big Rexploring shells up at us & they burst all around the plane- We call that "Flack"-
Haven't heard the results of the Osaka raid yet but suppose you have.
Here's a bit o' information you've been wanting. Jack is here on this island, a casualty in the Base Hospital. I just got word last nite about him but couldn't go down there at the time - If it all possible I'll see him today. Dear Mother and Sister,

What's the good word from home? Guess you got my letter of day before yesterday. I wrote it just before leaving for Kobe, Japan—Our plane was the last to get to the target & the last to leave. I stayed over the city of Kobe 2 hours while the 300 or more B-29s bombed—I saw the whole show from start to finish. There was plenty action & a movie could never be as interesting or exciting. I was flying for 17 hours & really tired when we returned—We leave in the evening & get back the next day at noon.

--- PAGE 13 ---
I don't know how busy I'm gonna be today. I'll stop now if nobody wants to go down to our place now tho'. If I don't fly we try to go to church today.

LATER:
Well I didn't fly, we cleaned our plane up good from the last mission. The service were Catholic this morning. I read some in the Bible and rested up instead of going to church. I just started to feel pretty good now so am going down to visit Jack again. I got few letters this morning with Hayleys enclosure. Sorry to hear grandpa is acting again. Suppose Miss Lovie Belle has gotten my letter to her about Jack already! Here's a clipping I got from Aldie. The plane's name is Battin Betty. Ours was Battin Betsy - We will probably

In the Marianas
March 29, 1945

Hello Mother & Sis,
Time to write a few lines before going to bed - Guess you got my last letter where I'd been to see Jack for the 2nd time?
Well we had a busy week last week. Our Group made it back on Japan in 10 days. So we got a day off yesterday.
Most everyone went to a picnic down on the beach - Army transportation and food was furnished. Everyone had a big time - I took a tour of the island myself. Went all around & saw Japs & dead Japs too. Went into some of the Pill Boxes where the dead bodies were still lying around dying up.

--- PAGE 19 ---
Found a few little souvenirs - quite we have been playing games. Checkers, buck gamu and ste-chess - We got a regular box of games in our supplies when we came across. They are coming in handy how - Each crew got Soft Balls, Foot Blls Jelly Balls etc.

How's Grandpa & John getting along? Got a letter from Mary. She said John was sick. I'll have to close for nite - We have a phonograph if some records in our hut now so music is playing(ly) When you send the sheets you can send me a few wash cloths too - If you've already sent the sheets that will be O.K. Tell all hello Love P.S. Heard from Paul Soler today - Austin

March 24, 1945

Hello Mother & all,

How's everything today? Guess you got my other letters - I haven't written for a couple days I don't think.

Have only a little time to write today! Just received word that Jack is being evacuated - Maybe he'll be in the state in a few days or in Hawaii anyway. He sent word for me to come see him. The last was impossible as I didn't get the word in time & was also busy & couldn't get away. Sure hope he gets to the state - He was going by air.

I got your air mail letters quicker, I like them much better. Which EB outfit is Mr. Albert Hutchinson in? Tell Mr. Willis that the fellow that was asking about him was just Mole Spy Commanding Officer.

--- PAGE 21 ---
Well all be off now - Will try to write more tomorrow sometime -
We washed our plane again this morning.
Glad for clear such sunny weather back there. Send me some clippings out of the paper once in a while.

Would have like to have visited Jack some more before he left. But guess he was in a hurry to leave here also.(H41)

Hope Grandpa is feeling better by now - Tell John Winn Hi -
Love to all
Austin

March 28, 1945
Marianas

5 missions
80 hours

Well, I've got to go now, guys. Will write you next time from Waycross, Ga.

You said that you'd gotten my letter yesterday? I haven't received it yet. You said that you had gotten Jack's letter on the 13th. Don't forget that she had learned about me by then. She was sure glad to hear from you. She left a note for me this morning, saying that she was going to see her doctor and that she was feeling better.

The last mission I was on was a daylight strike - eight planes. Information for next 16 hours. It's a real job - we get lots of change in the weather. We also like in the night. Bombs are new, too. Our bombardier and tail gunner both
