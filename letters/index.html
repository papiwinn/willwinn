The wartime letters of

Austin H. Winn

Cherry, Lauderdale County, Tennessee · 1943

Austin’s own words stay in the boxed letters, spelled as he wrote them. All added explanation is in green notes labeled Historical note. Home is the community of Cherry. Route 2, Henning is only the rural mail route on the envelopes.

Chapters

  • Chapter 1 Leaving Home January–March 1943 · train south, Miami Beach, emergency leave, orders to Oshkosh
  • Chapter 2 College in a Dairy Town April–June 1943 · 96th College Training Detachment, Oshkosh, first hours in the air
  • Chapter 3 Classification July–September 1943 · troop train west, Santa Ana, classified pilot, preflight
  • Chapter 4 Eagle Field October 1943 · Dos Palos, Ryan PT-22, first solo
  • Chapter 5 Sixty Hours November 1943 · Eagle Field, check rides, primary graduation
  • Chapter 6 The Vultee December 1943 · War Eagle Field, Lancaster, BT-13 basic
  • Chapter 7 Under the Hood January 1944 · War Eagle Field, night flying, the Link trainer
  • Chapter 8 Two Engines February–April 1944 · Marfa, AT-17, wings and bars
  • Chapter 9 Twenty Tons May–June 1944 · Roswell, B-17 transition
  • Chapter 10 The Pool July 1944 · Lincoln routing pool, Alamogordo crew school
Letter texts follow the family transcriptions at willwinn.xyz. Draft manuscript with historical notes, January 1943–July 1944.
