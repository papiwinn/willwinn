1944
MAAB
Apr 5th

Dear Mother and All,

How's everyone this morning, especially Sister & the Daly? I've gotta shoot Kello again today, that's Fort Sumter I think. I've finished all my flying time but still have to ride as Co-Pilot Thursday for Woolson as he is way behind! He's been sick & got behind.

I got my new uniform & tried it on - fits just swell. I haven't heard about the reservations yet, may get some news in the mail this morning.

We are having our graduation exercises April 14th now. I also hear there will be a program on the radio (N.B.C.) that day from here so try to tune in if possible. It may be as early as 8 A.M., but don't think so. Maybe you'll see it in the Radio Clock in the newspaper.

I guess John is getting lots of work done now with 2 Stardons. Does he still buy his gas wholesale?

--- PAGE 2 ---
I saw a nice show last nite. We did some tumbling exercises this morning. Well how's The little Kid - Write me about him. I guess you've already done so though it just hasn't gotten here yet.
I need to write Harvey Kinney a letter how he'll say Bye Bye.
Love & All,
Austin

--- PAGE 3 ---
Marta, Texas
April 10, 1944

Hello folks,

Well it's Monday noon now and we're having some of the worst dust storms I've ever seen. Boy you can't get out o' the house. We're going to a couple lectures this afternoon on combat experience - I'm getting my clothes all fitted up and think they're gonna look nice. We got some pictures like my class-book picture made, they ain't very good though she got a funny expression on my face. Guess we'll send cousin Wilbur one, he asked me to send him one when I graduated. The boys are already packing their bags for their furlough - we have some new flying equipment issued to us. We're sending my flying equipment on to my next base and spy parachute bag if I have a nice big case like the flyer had that supplies with us.

--- PAGE 4 ---
Morning,

Had a bad weather had to fly.

Glad to get away from this Texas heat. It's OK here as long as the wind doesn't blow too hard but boy you need a gas mask on to go anywhere today.

Guess I'll send out my graduation announcements in the morning. Boy did I wait until I was sure I'd graduate because nothing is very certain and only as you're a cadet there is always some chance you'll slip up somewhere & get washed out as they call it.

--- PAGE 5 ---
I guess we'll ride the train from Dallas as we never heard a word from the airlines since I had to change the reservations - Don't forget the radio program on April 14th.

We had a nice Easter service at church Sunday. We had a song and a little recitation from some of the Sunday School children - Little kids & boys about 6 or 10 yrs old.

Must say goodbye now - Love to all and see you soon

Cherio,
Austin

Dear Mother Hall,

I'm still in El Paso and have had a very nice time today. I visited Juarez Mexico this morning and saw a good show this afternoon. I'm leaving for Powell in the morning at 8:30 by Greyhound Bus. Will get there around 1 PM the same day. Guess you got the telegram. O.K., I had the nicest trip air OK, I had the nicest trip anywhere so nice and made anywhere so nice to comfortable also had my supper served on the plane (no extra charge) am staying in the Officers Club Dormitory here.

--- PAGE 9 ---
How's John Wines getting along? Hope he isn't getting too quasy. (Thx) He is really a fine baby, and I'm very proud to be his uncle. Thank John for the trip to Memphis - hope he did O.K. with the dog juices.

Better go now - ill mark a spot for Roswell on the back of the envelope so you can see where it is. (NORTH EAST OF El Paso)

Love to all
Austin

1947
Roswell, New Mexico
Wednesday Nite

A W
Hello Mother,
Just a note before going to bed tonight - I had a lovely trip from El Paso by bus - Really it was OK and staying in town tonight I will report in the morning. I went back to the jail this afternoon and it looks like a nice place Big too -
I went to a show tonight so I better take a shower and get in bed
Will write more when I settled down so don't expect to get too many letters in the next couple days.
Kello?
Love to all
Austin

--- PAGE 12 ---
Dear Mother,

What's new around home? I'm all fixed up here temporarily at least. I got all my papers filled out and cleared to the different offices that we had to go to upon reporting. I think I'll like it here fine. Since we are Officers we can go any-where when we aren't busy. Visit Roswell any nite if we feel like it. We'll probably be too tired for that very much though.

I got a vaccination for small-pox this morning. We have plenty of entertainment around here too. All types 700, 7 theatres.

It's almost 10 o'clock now & we'll have a busy day tomorrow. How's sister & her family? I'll need my trench coat as it's pretty cool here at Nite (or anyway you like).

The long pink coat. Send it Parcel Post. Insure for $50.00 Please! Thanks a lot.

Love,
2nd Lt Austin H Wynn O-774857 Austin
S/O Class 44D RAAF
Roswell, New Mexico

--- PAGE 13 ---
Dear Mother & Sister,

Hi there everyone, I'm back in school again at least for the first week it seems - we are going 7 hours a day including today (Sun.) and get 1 hour of P.T. I got wound yesterday at noon & I live about a mile from the flightline in what is known as the (B.O.Q.) Bachelor Officers Quarters. I walk over every morning to bring my books (which weigh about 20lbs) & stay all day till time for P.T., before going back to my quarters.

I'm with Wither who is with me, a boy from Oshkosh, Wis., that is a boy from Oshkosh - has been with me since Oshkosh. We have a Robert Winn here in my same class & section - he's from Los Angeles, Calif.

--- PAGE 14 ---
We are learning a lot about the B-17 before we ever get into one to fly it.

I saw John Edd Craig last nite. He still looks the same. I haven't run into Roy Crowder yet.

The food here is really the finest. Ice cream & pie almost every meal.

Has John written? I got a letter from you & sister yesterday. How did you get the telegram from me? I didn't know whether it would come by mail or telephone. I just called from the depot and it cost 40¢. I wrote Harvey King a letter tonight. It's about time I started toward home. I need to clean the place up a little & unpack some of my stuff. I got a letter from the Treasury Dept. wanting me to estimate the amount of income & victory tax I would pay for 1944. Has John filled out anything like that for me?

--- PAGE 15 ---
AW How's Oletta making it around there these days? Does she stay with you any - I guess you're still down at sisters a lot.

I hope the rains have let up a bit. I see the miss river is real high. I guess you've got backwater too now - Did Ing & Joe M really get married? Do they live at the State Farm?

Well I gotta go now. I must say I enjoyed my visit with you all more than you know. Wish I could come more often but will have to wait till we get this war under control.

Love to all, Austin
